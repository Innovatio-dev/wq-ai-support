# Update your profile details

Most profile details can be changed by you at any time. A few are tied to your verified identity and need our team.

## Edit your profile

1. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
2. The **General** tab opens.
3. Edit any of the following:
   - **Display name** (shown to your team in Network and Members)
   - **Profile photo** (JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB)
   - **Postal address**
   - **Time zone** (used for dates in your Back Office; commission periods always run on Pacific Time)
4. Select **Save changes**.

## Details you can't change yourself

| Detail | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Legal first and last name | Must match your verified identity and your PayQuicker account | Contact support with proof of the change |
| Date of birth | Part of your KYC record | Contact support |
| Country of residence | Affects verification and payouts | Contact support |
| Sponsor and tree position | Fixed at enrollment | Cannot be changed |

## Your profile photo

Use a clear photo of yourself or your business logo. Photos with income claims, cash, or luxury items used to imply earnings are removed under our earnings claims rules.

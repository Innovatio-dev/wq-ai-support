# Change language and accessibility settings

## Change the language

1. Select the **language flag** in the top bar of the Back Office.
2. Pick a language from the list.

The page reloads in the new language. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices. Emails from WealthQuest use the same language.

Legal documents (IBO Agreement, Compensation Plan, Policies and Procedures) are binding in their published version; translations are provided for convenience.

## Accessibility settings

1. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
2. Open the **Accessibility** tab.
3. Adjust:
   - **Text size**: Default, Large or Extra large
   - **Theme**: Light, Dark or match your device
   - **High contrast**
   - **Reduce motion** (turns off animations in charts and the Binary tree)
4. Changes apply immediately.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows): open global search
- **Esc**: close dialogs and search

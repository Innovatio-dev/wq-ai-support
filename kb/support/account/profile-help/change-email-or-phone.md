# Change your email address or mobile number

Your email is your login and where we send receipts and payout notices. Your mobile number is used for SMS verification.

## Change your email

1. Go to **Profile settings → General**.
2. Next to **Email**, select **Change**.
3. Enter your new email and your current password.
4. Enter the 6-digit code we send to the **new** address.
5. We also notify your **old** address, so you'll know if someone else tries this.

If you sign in with Google, changing your WealthQuest email does not change your Google account. Reconnect Google afterwards if the addresses differ; see [[account/logging-in/change-login-method]].

## Change your mobile number

1. Go to **Profile settings → General**.
2. Next to **Mobile number**, select **Change**.
3. Enter the new number, including country code.
4. Enter the SMS code we send to it.

Each mobile number can be verified on **one WealthQuest account only**. A number already used on another account is rejected.

## Didn't get the code?

- Email: check spam and wait up to 5 minutes, then select **Resend**. See also [[device-troubleshooting/notifications]].
- SMS: check the country code and that your phone can receive short-code texts.

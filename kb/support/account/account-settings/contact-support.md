# How to contact WealthQuest Support

## Find an answer yourself

Go to **WealthQuest Support** (or **Profile settings → Help center**) and choose:

- **Help with AI**: describe your problem in your own words and get an answer drawn from these help articles.
- **Basic search**: search articles by keyword, or browse categories.

## Open a ticket

1. Go to **Profile settings → Contact support**. If you can't log in, use **Contact support** on the WealthQuest Support site.
2. Pick a topic (Billing, Login, Verification, Payouts, Other).
3. Describe the issue and attach screenshots if you can.
4. Select **Submit**.

We reply **by email within 24 hours**. You can follow your ticket under **Contact support → My tickets**.

## Tips for a faster answer

- Write from the email address on your WealthQuest account.
- Include your member ID (shown under your name in Profile settings).
- For payment issues, include the invoice or transaction date.

## We will never ask for

Your password, 2FA codes, backup codes or full card number. Anyone asking for these is not WealthQuest. See [[safety-privacy/phishing-and-scams]].

# How to disable account

Disabling your account stops your license, signs you out everywhere and hides your profile from your team. It is not the same as deleting your data.

## Before you disable

- **Withdraw your cash balance** from the Wallet to PayQuicker. You can't log in to withdraw while disabled.
- **Download invoices** you may need for your records.
- If you're within **14 days** of enrollment, ask for a refund first; see [[payments-billing/refund-policy-14-days]].

## Disable your account

1. Go to **Profile settings → General**.
2. Scroll to **Disable account** and select it.
3. Read the summary of what happens, then enter your password.
4. Select **Disable account**.

## What happens

- Your monthly license is cancelled and no further charges are made.
- You are logged out on all devices and can't log in.
- Commissions already earned on sales made while you were current are still calculated and credited to your Wallet.
- **Compensation Gold Points** are never forfeited. **Reward Gold Points** are held for 90 days, then forfeited if you don't reactivate.
- Your tree position is kept while disabled. Positions are never transferred to anyone else.

## Reactivate

Contact support from the support site. Reactivation within 90 days restores your Reward Gold Points in full.

## Want your data deleted instead?

Disabling keeps your records. To request permanent deletion, see [[safety-privacy/data-export-and-deletion]].

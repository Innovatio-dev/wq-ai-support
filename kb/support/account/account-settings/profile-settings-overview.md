# A tour of Profile settings

Select your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the Back Office, then **Profile settings**. Settings are grouped into eight tabs.

## The tabs

| Tab | What you can do there |
|---|---|
| **General** | Name, photo, email, mobile number, address, time zone; disable your account |
| **Wallet** | See your cash and Gold Points balances; connect PayQuicker; withdraw |
| **Devices** | See where you're logged in; log out one or all devices |
| **Billing** | Payment cards, billing address and business details |
| **Security** | Password, sign-in methods (Google), two-factor authentication, backup codes |
| **Accessibility** | Text size, theme, contrast, reduce motion |
| **Contact support** | Open a ticket; view your open and past tickets |
| **Help center** | Search help articles or ask the AI assistant |

## Related places in the sidebar

Some account items live in the main sidebar rather than Profile settings:

- **Billing** (sidebar): subscription, renewal date, invoices, cancel license
- **Memberships**: your membership status and verification progress
- **Earnings** and **Transactions**: what you've earned and every Wallet movement

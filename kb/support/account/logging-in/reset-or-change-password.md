# How to reset or change your WealthQuest password

You can change your password any time from your Back Office, or reset it from the login page if you have forgotten it.

## Change your password (if you know your current password)

1. Log in to the Back Office.
2. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
3. Open the **Security** tab.
4. Under **Password**, select **Change password**.
5. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
6. Select **Save**.

Your new password must be at least 8 characters and include a lowercase letter, a number and a special character. Other devices stay signed in; to sign them out, use **Log out of all devices** on the same tab.

## Forgot your password?

1. Go to the WealthQuest login page and select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email from WealthQuest and select **Reset password**. The link works once and expires after 60 minutes.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it.
5. Log in with the new password. If you have two-factor authentication on, you will also be asked for your code. For your security, all other devices are signed out after a reset.

## Didn't get the reset email?

- Wait a few minutes and check spam, junk and promotions folders.
- Make sure you typed the email you enrolled with.
- If you normally sign in with **Google**, you have no WealthQuest password to reset; use **Continue with Google** instead.
- Request a new link. Only the most recent link works.

After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. Resetting your password does not unlock it sooner.

# How to log out of WealthQuest

Always log out when you use a shared or public computer.

## Log out on this device

1. Select your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the Back Office.
2. Select **Log out**.

You are returned to the login page. If you signed in with Google, this logs you out of WealthQuest only, not your Google account.

## Log out of all devices

1. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
2. Open the **Devices** tab.
3. Select **Log out of all devices**, or **Log out** next to a single device.

Every other session ends within a minute. You stay logged in on the device you are using.

## Automatic logout

For your security, WealthQuest logs you out after 30 days without activity on a device, or straight away after a password change made through **Forgot password?**.

If you see a device you don't recognize, log it out, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication. See [[safety-privacy/suspicious-login-hacked-account]].

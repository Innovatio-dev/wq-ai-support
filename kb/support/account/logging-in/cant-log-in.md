# Can't log in to WealthQuest

Work through these checks in order. Most login problems are solved by step 2 or 3.

## 1. Check how you signed up

WealthQuest supports two ways to log in:

- **Email and password**
- **Continue with Google**

If you enrolled with Google, there is no WealthQuest password. Select **Continue with Google** and pick the same Google account you used before.

## 2. Wrong password

1. Select **Forgot password?** on the login page.
2. Follow the email link to set a new password.

See [[account/logging-in/reset-or-change-password]].

## 3. Account locked

After **5 failed attempts**, your account is locked for **30 minutes**. Wait, then try once more with the correct password or reset it.

## 4. Two-factor code not accepted

- Make sure your phone's time is set automatically; authenticator codes depend on it.
- Use the newest code; each one is valid for 30 seconds.
- Lost your phone? Use a backup code. See [[account/security/lost-2fa-device]].

## 5. The login page won't load or keeps refreshing

- Try a private/incognito window.
- Clear cache and cookies for wealthquest.com.
- Use a supported browser.

See [[device-troubleshooting/app-not-loading]].

## 6. "Account disabled" or "suspended"

If you disabled your own account, contact support to reactivate it. A suspended account needs our team to review it; open a ticket from the support site.

## Still stuck?

Go to **WealthQuest Support → Contact support** and include your account email and a screenshot of any error. We reply by email within 24 hours.

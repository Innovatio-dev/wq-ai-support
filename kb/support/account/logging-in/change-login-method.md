# Changing how you log in to WealthQuest

You can log in with **email and password**, with **Google**, or have both connected so you always have a backup.

## Connect Google sign-in

1. Go to **Profile settings → Security**.
2. Under **Sign-in methods**, select **Connect Google**.
3. Choose your Google account and allow access.
4. Next time, select **Continue with Google** on the login page.

## Add a password (if you only use Google)

1. Go to **Profile settings → Security → Sign-in methods**.
2. Select **Set a password**.
3. We email you a link; follow it to create the password.

## Remove Google sign-in

1. Make sure you have a password set first; you can't remove your only sign-in method.
2. In **Sign-in methods**, select **Disconnect** next to Google.

## Change the email you log in with

Your login email is the email on your profile. See [[account/profile-help/change-email-or-phone]].

## Keep it secure

Whichever method you use, turn on two-factor authentication. If you sign in with Google, also protect your Google account with 2-Step Verification.

# Using the Binary tree

The **Binary tree** shows your placement tree: every member sits in exactly one position with a **left leg** and a **right leg** beneath it. It's the tree that decides your weak-leg volume and your rank.

## Find your way around

1. Open **Binary tree** in the sidebar. You're at the top.
2. Drag to pan; use **+ / −** or pinch to zoom.
3. Select a member card to see their name, status, current rank, and left/right volume.
4. Select **Go to top** to return to your own position, or search a member with **⌘K**.

## The numbers on your card

- **Left BV / Right BV**: Business Volume in each leg over the rolling last 31 days.
- **Weak leg**: whichever leg has less volume today. It can switch from day to day.
- Volume leaves the 31-day window on the 32nd day and can't be carried forward.

## Colors and badges

- **Green**: Active or Qualified
- **Grey**: Pending or Paused
- **Dashed outline**: reserved position for an Applicant still verifying
- **Empty slot**: open position

## Binary tree vs. Network

The Binary tree shows **placement**. Who personally enrolled whom is shown in **Network**; see [[in-app-features/network-and-members]].

Placements are final: positions can't be moved, swapped, sold or transferred.

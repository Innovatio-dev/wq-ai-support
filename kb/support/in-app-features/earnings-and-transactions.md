# Earnings, Bonuses and Transactions pages

## Earnings

**Earnings** shows your totals per commission week (Tuesday–Monday, Pacific Time), with the cash and Gold Points parts. The current week is marked **In progress** until it closes on Monday at 11:59 p.m. PT; amounts are credited on Friday at 12:00 a.m. PT.

The **Dashboard** shows the same week-to-date figure at a glance.

## Bonuses

**Bonuses** splits earnings by income stream, for example Direct Bonus, Stable Income Team Bonus, Generational Match, Customer Launch Bonus, Fast 5 Bonus and Leadership Rank Bonus. Select a row to see the sales or days it was calculated from.

Amounts shown before Friday are provisional; they're confirmed when verification completes.

## Transactions

**Transactions** is the full ledger of your Wallet:

1. Filter by type: **Payout**, **Withdrawal**, **Gold Points redemption**, **Adjustment**, **Held**.
2. Select a row for details.

Every **adjustment** (for example, a refunded sale) is linked to the order that caused it, so you can trace any change.

## Why an amount can change

- A sale is refunded or charged back: its bonuses are reversed.
- Rank is recalculated because volume was reversed.
- An amount is **held** while a company-wide payout cap applies; it's released in a later week, oldest first.

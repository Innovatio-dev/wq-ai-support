# Understanding the Rank Progress page

Ranks run from **R1 Bronze Associate** to **R12 Gold Ambassador**. Only Qualified members can hold a rank.

## What the page shows

1. **Today's rank**: your Daily Rank Snapshot, calculated at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time from the last 31 days.
2. **This week's highest rank**: your Weekly Rank for the current commission week (Tuesday to Monday, Pacific Time).
3. **Next rank checklist**:
   - **Weak-leg BV** needed vs. what you have
   - **Personally sponsored Active members** needed on the left and right
   - For higher ranks, **team members at a given rank** or **leadership lines**
4. **Volume expiring soon**: BV that will leave your 31-day window in the next 7 days.

## Why rank can go down

Rank is re-earned every day. It drops when:

- volume from 31+ days ago leaves the window,
- a personally sponsored member stops being Active,
- a sale is refunded or charged back, or
- your license lapses.

You must meet **every** requirement of a rank, and of all ranks below it, on the same day. There's no partial rank.

## Full requirements

The exact volume and structure for each rank are in the Compensation Plan (section 6); see [[membership/compensation-plan-guide]]. Rank describes eligibility, not a promised income.

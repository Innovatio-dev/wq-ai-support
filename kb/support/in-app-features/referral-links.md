# Share your referral links

Your referral link is how a sale is credited to you. A sale is always credited to **one** member only, based on the link used, then the customer's existing assignment.

## Your links

| Link | Use it for | Available from |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer link** (WealthVentures) | Customers buying a product | Pending status, after accepting the Product Appendix |
| **Enrollment link** | People who want to join as members | Active status |

## Copy a link

1. Open **Referral links** in the sidebar, or select **Share link** in the top bar.
2. Pick the link type.
3. Select **Copy**, **Show QR code**, or a share button (email, WhatsApp, Messenger).

## Track results

Each link shows clicks, sign-ups and verified sales over the last 31 days. Sales appear once payment is verified, usually within minutes.

## Rules when sharing

- Promote only product lines whose Product Appendix you've accepted. At launch that's WealthVentures only.
- Never make income or lifestyle claims when sharing a link; see [[safety-privacy/earnings-claims-rules]].
- Don't buy through your own link. Personal purchases don't count as sales.
- Don't pay for ads using the WealthQuest name unless the Policies and Procedures allow it.

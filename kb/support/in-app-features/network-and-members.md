# Network and Members: see your team

WealthQuest keeps two separate views of your team.

## Network (enroller tree)

**Network** shows who personally enrolled whom, starting from you. It's used for your personally sponsored count, leadership lines and the Generational Match.

1. Open **Network** in the sidebar.
2. Expand a member to see the people they personally enrolled.
3. Each row shows status, current rank and join date.

## Members (list view)

**Members** is a table of everyone in your organization.

1. Open **Members** in the sidebar.
2. Filter by **status** (Applicant, Pending, Active, Qualified, Paused), **rank**, **leg** or **personally sponsored**.
3. Sort by join date or rank.
4. Select **Export CSV** to download the current view.

## Privacy

You see team members' display names, statuses and ranks, not their contact details, earnings or ID documents. Use exported lists only to support your own team, in line with the Privacy Policy.

## Network vs. Binary tree

| | Network | Binary tree |
|---|---|---|
| Shows | Who enrolled whom | Where each member is placed |
| Used for | Personally sponsored counts, leadership lines, Generational Match | Weak-leg volume and rank |

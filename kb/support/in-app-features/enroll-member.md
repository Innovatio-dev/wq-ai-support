# Enroll a new member

You can enroll new members once you're **Active**. Enrolling someone earns you nothing by itself; bonuses only ever come from real sales to customers.

## Enroll someone

1. Select **Enroll member** in the top bar or sidebar.
2. Enter the new member's name, email and country.
3. Choose a **placement**: the leg (left or right) and position in your Binary tree.
4. Select **Send invitation**. They get an email to accept the IBO Agreement, verify their mobile number by SMS and pay.
5. They then complete identity verification. Their position is reserved from payment.

Or simply share your enrollment link; see [[in-app-features/referral-links]].

## Placement rules

- Every member gets exactly **one** position, and it can't be changed later.
- You can't place a personally sponsored member on the **inside leg** of the team member receiving them. Place them on that team member's outside leg instead. The system rejects inside-leg placements.
- Each person may hold only one account, verified with their own identity and a unique mobile number.

## Before you enroll someone

- Share the Compensation Plan and Income Disclosure Statement, and never promise income.
- Tell them about the $99 activation and $29 monthly license fees, and the 14-day full refund.

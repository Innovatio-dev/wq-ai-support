# Gold Points basics

Gold Points (GP) work like airline miles: a reward you can put toward a later WealthQuest purchase. **1 Gold Point = $1 of redemption value.**

## Where Gold Points come from

- **Compensation Gold Points**: 10% of every commission payment. They **never expire** and are never forfeited, even if you cancel.
- **Reward Gold Points**: issued with WealthVentures membership payments and promotions. If your account lapses, they're held for **90 days** and restored in full if you reactivate in time.
- **Refer 4 award**: issued as an **activation code** that expires **40 days** after issue.

## What you can use them for

- A WealthQuest subscription (limit: **1 WealthVentures subscription per calendar month**)
- WealthQuest products
- Upgrades
- Event tickets

## Rules

- **No cash value.** Gold Points can never be withdrawn, cashed out or converted to money or crypto.
- **All or nothing.** If the order costs more than your available Gold Points, you can't use them on that order.
- **Not transferable** to anyone, for any reason.
- Orders paid with Gold Points generate no BV and no commission.

## Redeem Gold Points

1. Go to **Shop** and add the item.
2. At checkout, select **Pay with Gold Points**.

## Not related to GoldMind or Liquid Gold

Gold Points have nothing to do with GoldMind or Liquid Gold, which aren't live. Gold Points aren't linked to the price of gold, any commodity or any cryptocurrency.

# Your Wallet and withdrawing to PayQuicker

## When you're paid

- Commission weeks run **Tuesday 12:00 a.m. to Monday 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time**.
- Earnings for the week are credited to your Wallet on **Friday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time**.
- Every payment is split **90% cash** (US dollars) and **10% Gold Points**.

## Your three balances

Go to **Profile settings → Wallet**:

| Balance | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Withdrawable to PayQuicker |
| **Compensation Gold Points** | Your 10% share; never expire |
| **Reward Gold Points** | Issued with membership payments and promotions |

## Withdraw cash

1. Make sure your identity is verified with WealthQuest **and** PayQuicker, under the same name.
2. In **Wallet**, select **Withdraw**.
3. Enter the amount and confirm with your 2FA code if enabled.
4. The money moves to your PayQuicker account. From there, spend or transfer it using PayQuicker's options.

If you need a local currency, PayQuicker converts once, at its rate at the time of withdrawal. PayQuicker's own fees, minimums and limits are shown when you set up your PayQuicker account.

Gold Points can't be withdrawn; they stay in the Back Office.

## Holds and adjustments

- A refunded or charged-back sale reverses the commission on it.
- If reversals exceed what you're owed, the difference is a **Negative Balance**, offset against future earnings. There's no interest and no invoice.
- You're responsible for taxes on your earnings.

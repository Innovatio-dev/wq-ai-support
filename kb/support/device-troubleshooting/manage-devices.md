# See and manage your logged-in devices

## View your devices

1. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
2. Open the **Devices** tab.

Each entry shows:

- Device and browser (for example, *Chrome on Windows*)
- Approximate location, based on IP address
- Last active time
- **This device** label on the one you're using
- **Trusted** badge if you ticked "Trust this device for 30 days" at 2FA

## Log out a device

- Select **Log out** next to a device, or
- Select **Log out of all devices** to end every session except this one.

## Remove a trusted device

Select **⋯ → Remove trust**. That device will ask for a 2FA code next time.

## If you don't recognize a device

1. Log it out straight away.
2. Change your password.
3. Turn on two-factor authentication.
4. Check **Profile settings → Wallet** and **Transactions** for anything unexpected.

Locations are approximate; a VPN or mobile network can show a nearby city or a different region.

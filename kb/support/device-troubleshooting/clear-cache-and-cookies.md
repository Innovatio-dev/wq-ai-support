# Clear your browser cache and cookies

Clearing the cache removes stored copies of pages; clearing cookies logs you out. You'll need to log in again afterwards.

## Chrome and Edge (desktop)

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Windows) or **⌘+Shift+Delete** (Mac).
2. Set **Time range** to **All time**.
3. Tick **Cookies and other site data** and **Cached images and files**.
4. Select **Clear data**.

## Safari (Mac)

1. **Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data**.
2. Search **wealthquest**, select it and choose **Remove**.
3. Then **Develop → Empty Caches** (enable the Develop menu in **Settings → Advanced** if needed).

## Firefox (desktop)

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Windows) or **⌘+Shift+Delete** (Mac).
2. Choose **Everything**, tick **Cookies** and **Cache**, then **Clear**.

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

**Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data**, search **wealthquest**, swipe left and **Delete**.

## Android (Chrome)

**⋮ → Settings → Site settings → All sites**, find **wealthquest.com**, then **Delete / Clear & reset**.

## After clearing

Close and reopen the browser, then log in again. If you use two-factor authentication, you'll be asked for a code because trusted-device cookies were removed.

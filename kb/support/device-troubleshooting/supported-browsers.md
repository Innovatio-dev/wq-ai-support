# Supported browsers and devices

The WealthQuest Back Office runs in your web browser. There's nothing to install.

## Supported browsers

We support the **latest two major versions** of:

| Browser | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Google Chrome | Windows, macOS, Linux | Android, iOS |
| Safari | macOS | iOS / iPadOS |
| Microsoft Edge | Windows, macOS | Android, iOS |
| Mozilla Firefox | Windows, macOS, Linux | Android |

Internet Explorer and very old browser versions aren't supported.

## Check your browser version

- **Chrome / Edge**: menu **⋮ → Help → About**
- **Safari**: **Safari → About Safari** (updates come with macOS / iOS updates)
- **Firefox**: **☰ → Help → About Firefox**

## Screen size

The Back Office works on phones, but the **Binary tree** and **Network** are easiest to use on a tablet or computer (at least 1024 px wide).

## Add it to your home screen

On mobile, open the Back Office and choose **Add to Home Screen** from the share or browser menu for one-tap access.

## Required settings

- JavaScript and cookies must be enabled for wealthquest.com.
- Allow camera access during identity verification so you can photograph your ID and take a selfie.

# Manage notifications

## Where notifications appear

- **Bell icon** in the top bar: new team members, rank changes, payouts, verification updates.
- **Email**: payouts, invoices, failed payments, security alerts.
- **Browser notifications** (optional): the same alerts as the bell, even when the tab is in the background.

## Choose what you get

1. Go to **Profile settings → General → Notifications**.
2. Turn each type on or off for **In-app**, **Email** and **Browser**:
   - Team activity (new enrollments, rank changes in your team)
   - Earnings and payouts
   - Product and event news
3. Changes save automatically.

**Security and billing emails can't be turned off**: new sign-ins, password changes, failed payments and receipts.

## Turn on browser notifications

1. In **Notifications**, select **Enable browser notifications**.
2. Choose **Allow** when your browser asks.

If you chose **Block** before, allow notifications for wealthquest.com in your browser's site settings.

## Not receiving emails?

- Check spam, junk and promotions, and mark WealthQuest as **Not spam**.
- Add no-reply@wealthquest.com to your contacts.
- Confirm your email in **Profile settings → General**.

Real WealthQuest emails never ask for your password or 2FA codes.

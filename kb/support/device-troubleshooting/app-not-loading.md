# Back Office not loading or showing errors

Try these steps in order, checking after each one.

## 1. Reload

Press **⌘R** (Mac) or **Ctrl+R** / **F5** (Windows). For a hard reload, add **Shift**.

## 2. Check your connection

Open another website. If it's slow too, restart your router or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

## 3. Check for a service notice

The WealthQuest Support site shows a banner during planned maintenance or known issues. Commission calculation runs after the week closes on Monday at 11:59 p.m. PT, and some pages can be slower on Monday night and Tuesday morning.

## 4. Open a private window

Open a private/incognito window and log in. If it works there, the problem is your cache or an extension.

## 5. Clear cache and cookies

See [[device-troubleshooting/clear-cache-and-cookies]].

## 6. Turn off extensions

Ad blockers, privacy and VPN extensions can block parts of the Back Office such as the Binary tree or the Sumsub verification screen. Pause them for wealthquest.com.

## 7. Update or switch browser

Make sure you're on a supported browser; see [[device-troubleshooting/supported-browsers]].

## Still not working?

Open a ticket with:

- a screenshot including any **reference code**,
- your browser and version, device and operating system,
- the page you were on and the time it happened.

# Suspicious login or hacked account

We email you whenever your account is signed in from a new device. If it wasn't you, act straight away.

## If you can still log in

1. **Log out all devices**: **Profile settings → Devices → Log out of all devices**.
2. **Change your password**: **Profile settings → Security**. Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
3. **Turn on two-factor authentication**, or reset it if it was already on.
4. **Check your details**: email and mobile in **General**, sign-in methods in **Security**.
5. **Check money movements**: **Wallet** and **Transactions** for withdrawals you didn't make.

If you use Google sign-in, also secure your Google account.

## If you can't log in

1. Try **Forgot password?** on the login page.
2. If your email was changed, check your **old** inbox for our "email changed" notice and follow its **This wasn't me** link.
3. Open a ticket on the WealthQuest Support site under **Login → Account compromised**. We'll lock the account while we verify your identity.

## What happens to your money

Withdrawals need a verified identity that matches your PayQuicker account, which makes cash-out by someone else difficult. Tell us about any withdrawal you didn't make as soon as possible.

## Prevent it happening again

- Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app.
- Never share your password or codes. WealthQuest staff will never ask for them.
- Watch out for fake login pages; see [[safety-privacy/phishing-and-scams]].

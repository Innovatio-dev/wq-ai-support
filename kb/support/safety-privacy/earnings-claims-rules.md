# Rules for talking about earnings

WealthQuest is newly launched and has **no earnings history**. No one can honestly say what a member typically earns, so income claims are tightly limited.

## The rule

Any statement about earnings must be consistent with, and supported by, the **Income Disclosure Statement**. The full rules are in the Income Disclosure Statement and **section 4.2 of the Policies and Procedures**.

## Don't

- Promise or suggest any income ("replace your salary", "earn $5,000 a month").
- Present Compensation Plan tables or examples as what people will earn. They only show how the arithmetic works.
- Show cash, screenshots of earnings, cars, travel or luxury items to imply income.
- Say rank, bonuses or travel awards are easy, typical or guaranteed.
- Say anyone earns money for recruiting. Compensation comes only from sales to genuine customers.
- Describe Gold Points as cash, an investment or linked to gold or crypto.

## Do

- Talk about the products and why you like them.
- Say clearly that income isn't guaranteed and most people in direct selling earn little or nothing, with some losing money after fees.
- Point people to the Income Disclosure Statement and Compensation Plan.
- Mention the $99 + $29/month fees and the 14-day full refund.

## When in doubt

Leave it out, or ask support before posting. Breaking these rules can lead to a compliance directive, which affects Good Standing.

# Download or delete your personal data (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and, in many cases, delete your personal data. The **Privacy Policy** explains what we collect and why.

## Download a copy of your data

1. Go to **Profile settings → Contact support**.
2. Choose **Privacy → Export my data**.
3. Confirm with your password (and 2FA code if on).

We email a secure download link, usually within a few days and no later than 30 days. It includes your profile, membership history, sales and earnings records, invoices and support tickets. The link expires after 7 days.

## Correct your data

Update most details yourself in **Profile settings → General**. For your legal name or date of birth, contact support.

## Delete your data

1. Withdraw any cash balance and download anything you need.
2. Go to **Profile settings → Contact support → Privacy → Delete my data**.
3. We confirm your identity and reply by email.

Deletion is **permanent** and closes your account. Your tree position stays in the structure as an empty position.

## What we must keep

Some records are kept for the period the law requires, even after deletion: invoices and payment records, tax records of commissions paid, and identity (KYC/AML) records. These are restricted and used only for legal purposes.

Identity documents are processed by **Sumsub** and **PayQuicker**, who hold their own records under their privacy policies.

## Only need a break?

Disabling your account keeps your data and can be undone; see [[account/account-settings/disable-account]].

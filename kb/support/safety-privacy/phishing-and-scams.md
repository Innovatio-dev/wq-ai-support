# Spot phishing and scams

## WealthQuest will never

- ask for your **password**, **2FA code** or **backup codes**,
- ask for your **full card number** by email, chat, phone or social media,
- ask you to pay fees to "unlock" or "release" a payout,
- sell, buy or transfer **Gold Points** (they can't be transferred),
- offer crypto, gold or "Liquid Gold / GoldMind" investment deals.

## Signs of a fake message

- The sender or link isn't on **wealthquest.com** (look closely for lookalikes such as *wea1thquest* or *wealthquest-support.net*).
- Urgent threats: "your account will be closed in 1 hour".
- Promises of guaranteed income, bonuses or rank upgrades.
- Attachments you weren't expecting.

## Check before you click

1. Don't use the link in the message. Type **wealthquest.com** yourself or use a bookmark.
2. Log in and check your **notifications** (bell icon). Real account alerts appear there too.
3. Unsure? Ask on the WealthQuest Support site with **Help with AI** or open a ticket.

## If you clicked or shared something

1. Change your password straight away.
2. Log out all devices and turn on 2FA; see [[safety-privacy/suspicious-login-hacked-account]].
3. If you gave card details, call your bank.
4. Forward the message to support so we can warn others.

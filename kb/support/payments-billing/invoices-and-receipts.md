# Find and download your invoices

An invoice is created for every fee you pay: the one-time activation fee and each monthly license payment.

## Download an invoice

1. Open **Billing** in the Back Office sidebar.
2. Scroll to **Invoices**. Each row shows the date, description, amount and status (Paid, Failed, Refunded).
3. Select the **download** icon on a row to save the PDF.

A copy of each paid invoice is also emailed to the address on your account.

## Add your business details

To show a company name, address or tax ID on future invoices:

1. Go to **Profile settings → Billing**.
2. Edit **Billing address and business details**.
3. Save. The details appear on all invoices issued from now on.

Invoices already issued cannot be edited in the Back Office.

## Invoices vs. earnings statements

Invoices cover what you **pay** WealthQuest. What WealthQuest **pays you** (commissions and bonuses) is listed under **Earnings** and **Transactions**; see [[in-app-features/earnings-and-transactions]].

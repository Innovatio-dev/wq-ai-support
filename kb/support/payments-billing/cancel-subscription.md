# Cancel your monthly license

You can cancel your $29 monthly license at any time. Cancelling is as simple as enrolling.

## How to cancel

1. Open **Billing** in the Back Office sidebar.
2. Under **Subscription**, select **Cancel license**.
3. Choose a reason (optional) and select **Confirm cancellation**.
4. You receive a confirmation email.

Cancellation takes effect at the **next billing cycle**. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.

If you are within 14 days of enrollment, you can also ask for a full refund; see [[payments-billing/refund-policy-14-days]].

## What happens after cancellation

- Your status becomes **Enrolled — Paused Activation** when the paid period ends. You earn nothing new while paused.
- Commissions earned on sales made while your license was current are **still paid**.
- **Compensation Gold Points** never expire and are never forfeited.
- **Reward Gold Points** are held for 90 days and restored if you reactivate within that window.
- Your tree position, sales history and rank record are kept.

## Restart later

Go to **Billing → Reactivate license**. Your status returns as soon as payment succeeds.

Cancelling your license does not close your login. To stop using your account entirely, see [[account/account-settings/disable-account]].

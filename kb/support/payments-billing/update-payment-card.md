# Update your payment card

Your monthly license is charged to the default card in your account. Update it before it expires to avoid a failed payment.

## Add or replace a card

1. Select your avatar, then **Profile settings**.
2. Open the **Billing** tab.
3. Under **Payment methods**, select **Add card**.
4. Enter the card details and billing address, then select **Save**. Your bank may ask you to confirm with a one-time code.
5. Select **Set as default** on the new card.

## Remove a card

1. In **Profile settings → Billing**, find the card.
2. Select **⋯ → Remove**.

You cannot remove your only default card while your license is active. Add a new card first, or cancel your license.

## After a failed payment

If a payment already failed, select **Retry payment** after updating your card. You have 24 hours from the failure to fix it; see [[payments-billing/failed-payment-grace-period]].

We never ask for card details by email, chat or phone. See [[safety-privacy/phishing-and-scams]].

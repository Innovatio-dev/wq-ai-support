# WealthQuest fees: activation and monthly license

Running a WealthQuest business has two fees. Both are paid to WealthQuest and neither one earns anyone a commission.

## The two fees

| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Activation fee | $99, one time | At enrollment |
| Monthly license fee | $29 per month | At enrollment, then every month on the same date |

**The $99 activation fee** covers the Welcome onboarding video, setup and connection of all WealthQuest apps to your account, and the Exclusive Leadership Video, which appears in your Back Office shortly after activation.

**The $29 monthly license** covers that month's access to WealthQuest tools, the invoicing system, the Binary tree and Network views, and that month's allotment of tool tokens.

## Good to know

- The license **renews automatically** each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time; it takes effect for the next billing cycle.
- New members have **14 days** from enrollment to cancel for any reason and get a full refund.
- While your identity verification is pending (Applicant status), further monthly billing is **paused** and resumes once you are verified.
- Fees are **not commissionable**: they generate no Business Volume (BV), count toward no rank and pay no one a bonus.

## See your charges

1. Open **Billing** in the Back Office sidebar.
2. Your next renewal date and amount are shown at the top.
3. Past charges are listed under **Invoices**.

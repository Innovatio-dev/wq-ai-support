# What happens if a payment fails

If your monthly license payment fails, nothing changes straight away. You get a **24-hour grace period** to fix it.

## During the 24-hour grace period

- You keep your full Active or Qualified status.
- Sales made during the grace period still count and generate BV as normal.
- We email you and show a banner in the Back Office.

## Fix a failed payment

1. Go to **Profile settings → Billing**.
2. Update the card or add a new one (see [[payments-billing/update-payment-card]]).
3. Select **Retry payment**. The banner clears once the payment succeeds.

Common reasons a card is declined: expired card, insufficient funds, or your bank blocking an international or recurring charge. Call your bank if the retry fails again.

## If the grace period ends unpaid

Your status changes to **Enrolled — Paused Activation**:

- You earn nothing new while paused.
- Your account, verification (KYC) record, tree position, sales history and rank are **kept**.
- Commissions you already earned on sales made while you were current are still paid.
- Reward Gold Points are held for 90 days; Compensation Gold Points are never lost.

As soon as a payment goes through, your status comes back automatically with no re-qualification. Your rank may take a while to recover, because rank is based on a rolling 31-day volume total and no volume counted while you were paused.

The grace period does not apply if you cancel your license yourself.

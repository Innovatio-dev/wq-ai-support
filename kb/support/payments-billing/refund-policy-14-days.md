# Requesting a refund within 14 days

Every new WealthQuest member has **14 days from enrollment** to cancel for any reason and receive a **full refund** of the fees paid (activation fee and monthly license).

## How to request a refund

1. Go to **Profile settings → Contact support**.
2. Choose **Billing → Refund request**.
3. Include the email on your account and the date you enrolled.
4. Submit. Our team replies by email within 24 hours.

Once approved, the refund goes back to the original card. Most banks show it within 5–10 business days.

## Declined applications

If your identity verification does not clear within 30 days, your application is automatically declined and the declined-application refund rule in your IBO Agreement applies. See [[membership/kyc-verification]].

## After 14 days

After the 14-day window, fees already paid are generally not refundable. You can still cancel at any time to stop future charges; see [[payments-billing/cancel-subscription]].

## What happens to earnings

If you requested a refund after making sales, any commission paid on a sale that is itself later refunded is reversed. Commissions on sales that stand are not affected by your own refund.

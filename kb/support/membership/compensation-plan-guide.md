# Where to find the Compensation Plan

The **WealthQuest Compensation Plan Agreement** (effective 10.08.2026) sets out every rule, threshold and amount that decides what you're paid. Help articles give short pointers only; when in doubt, the Plan is what counts.

## Where to read it

- In the Back Office: **Assets → Legal documents → Compensation Plan**
- Online: https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/compensation-plan

Read it together with the IBO Agreement, the Policies and Procedures, the Income Disclosure Statement and the Privacy Policy, all under **Assets → Legal documents**.

## The Plan at a glance

- All compensation comes from sales to genuine customers. Nothing is paid for enrolling or recruiting anyone.
- Fees ($99 activation, $29/month) are never commissionable.
- There are eight income streams, including the Direct Bonus (10% of BV on your own verified retail sales) and rank-based team bonuses.
- Commission weeks run **Tuesday 12:00 a.m. to Monday 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time**, paid to your Wallet on **Friday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time**.
- Payments are **90% cash and 10% Gold Points**.

## No income is guaranteed

WealthQuest is newly launched and has no earnings history. Figures in the Plan show how the arithmetic works; they are not projections. Most people in direct selling earn little or nothing, and some lose money after fees. Read the **Income Disclosure Statement** before relying on any number.

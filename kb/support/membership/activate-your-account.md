# Become Active: accept the Product Appendix and make your first sale

Paying your fees doesn't make you eligible to earn. Two steps do.

## Step 1: Accept the WealthVentures Product Appendix

WealthVentures is the only live product line at launch. You can't promote it, and its sales don't count for you, until you accept its Product Appendix.

1. Open **Products** in the sidebar.
2. Select **WealthVentures → Review Product Appendix**.
3. Read it, tick the confirmation, and select **Accept**.

GoldMind and Liquid Gold are not live and can't be promoted or sold.

## Step 2: Make your first verified retail sale

1. Open **Referral links** and copy your WealthVentures link.
2. Share it with a genuine customer who wants the product.
3. The customer buys through your link and payment is verified.

That sale moves you from **Pending** to **Active** and is itself commissionable: it earns a Direct Bonus like every sale after it. It also unlocks enrolling new members.

## What counts as a qualifying sale

A completed, paid, verified sale through your link to a **Verified/Independent Customer**: someone who isn't a WealthQuest member or Brand Advocate, isn't your spouse or household member, and isn't buying mainly to help you qualify. Your own purchases never count, and neither do orders paid with Gold Points.

If the activating sale is refunded or charged back, the bonus is reversed and your status returns to Pending until a new qualifying sale.

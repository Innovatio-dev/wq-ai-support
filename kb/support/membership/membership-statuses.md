# Membership statuses explained

Your **status** shows where you are in getting set up and active. It is separate from your **rank** (R1–R12), which you'll find on the Rank Progress page. See your status under **Memberships** in the sidebar.

## The statuses

| Status | What it means | What you can do | How to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Applicant** | You've paid, but identity verification hasn't cleared | Complete verification only | Pass both Sumsub and PayQuicker checks |
| **Pending** (Enrolled — Pending Activation) | Verified and onboarded, no verified sale yet | Training and tools; you can't enroll others or earn | Make your first verified retail sale |
| **Active** | License current and at least one verified retail sale | Earn the Direct Bonus and customer bonuses; enroll members | Make one verified sale on each leg of your Binary tree |
| **Qualified** | Active, plus a verified sale on your left and your right leg | Eligible for rank-based bonuses, depending on your rank | Grow your rank on Rank Progress |
| **Paused** (Enrolled — Paused Activation) | Your monthly license lapsed | Nothing new is earned; everything is kept | Pay your license; status returns automatically |

## Important details

- **Applicant** lasts up to **30 days**. If verification hasn't cleared by then, the application is declined. Monthly billing is paused while you're an Applicant.
- **Your first sale pays.** The sale that makes you Active earns a Direct Bonus like any other.
- Only sales to genuine customers count: not yourself, your spouse or household, other members, or someone buying just to help you qualify.
- Active and Qualified are **not permanent**. They switch off while your license is lapsed and switch back on as soon as it's paid.

Your status tells you what you're eligible for, not what you'll earn. Earnings depend on real sales and are never guaranteed.

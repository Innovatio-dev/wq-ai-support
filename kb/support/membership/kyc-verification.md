# Verify your identity (KYC)

Everyone who joins WealthQuest completes identity verification ("Know Your Customer", or KYC). Until it clears, you're an **Applicant**: no Back Office access beyond verification, no volume and no earnings. Your place in the Binary tree is reserved from the moment you paid.

## Two checks, both required

1. **WealthQuest check, by Sumsub**: photo ID and a selfie.
2. **PayQuicker check**: PayQuicker, our payout provider, runs its own independent check when you open your payout account.

You become verified only when **both** have cleared. WealthQuest does not share its Sumsub result with PayQuicker; we share only your name, email and country.

## Complete verification

1. Log in. You'll land on the verification screen (or go to **Memberships**).
2. Select **Start identity check** and follow the Sumsub steps: choose your country and ID type, photograph the front and back, then take a selfie.
3. Select **Open PayQuicker account** and complete PayQuicker's steps using the **same identity**.
4. Check progress on **Memberships**. Most checks finish within minutes; some take up to 48 hours.

## Tips to pass first time

- Use a valid, unexpired government ID.
- Good light, no glare, all four corners visible.
- Your legal name must match on your ID, WealthQuest and PayQuicker.

## Deadline

You have **30 days**. If both checks haven't cleared by then, the application is declined and the declined-application refund rule in your IBO Agreement applies. Monthly billing is paused until you're verified.

## One account per person

Each identity and mobile number can verify only one WealthQuest account.

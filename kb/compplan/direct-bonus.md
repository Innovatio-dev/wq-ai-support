# Direct Bonus

## §8.1 What it pays

An Active IBO earns **10% of the Business Volume** of every personally generated, commissionable retail sale. It requires no team, no Rank and no structure — only a sale.

## §8.2 Eligibility

- You must hold Active IBO status at the time of the transaction — or the transaction must itself be the sale that confers Active IBO status on you.
- Your Monthly License Fee must be current at the time of the transaction.
- You must have affirmatively accepted the Product Appendix for the product line sold (§2.3).

**Your first sale is commissionable.** The verified retail sale that moves you from Enrolled — Pending Activation to Active IBO earns a Direct Bonus in the ordinary way, on the same 10% of BV as every sale after it. You are not required to make one uncompensated sale to unlock compensation: the sale that activates you pays.

This is the only point in the Plan at which the Direct Bonus looks at the moment immediately after a transaction rather than the moment immediately before it, and it applies to the activating sale alone. If that sale is later refunded or charged back within the applicable return window, the Direct Bonus paid on it is reversed under Section 16, and Active IBO status reverts to Enrolled — Pending Activation until a new qualifying sale is made (Agreement, Section 4.4).

## §8.3 What counts as a Commissionable Event

A Direct Bonus is generated when a Customer order is completed and paid, the order contains commissionable BV, and you are the credited seller.

## §8.4 Attribution

Each transaction generates a Direct Bonus for **one** qualified IBO only. Attribution is determined, in order, by:
1. the referral link used,
2. the Customer's standing assignment to an IBO, and
3. the system's tracking rules.

A transaction is never split between two IBOs, and a Customer is never assigned to more than one IBO at a time.

## §8.5 Exclusions

No Direct Bonus is paid on the Activation Fee, the Monthly License Fee, administrative or shipping charges, any item identified as non-commissionable in the Product & BV Schedule, an IBO's own personal purchase, or any order paid in whole or in part with Gold Points.

## §8.6 Effect of a later lapse

A Direct Bonus earned on a sale made while your Monthly License Fee was current remains payable even if your License lapses before the Payout Date. A lapse stops future accrual; it does not claw back compensation already earned. The only basis on which earned compensation is reversed is a refund or chargeback of the underlying sale (Agreement, Section 8, and Section 16).

## §8.7 Payout

Direct Bonuses are calculated at the close of the Weekly Commission Period in which the sale completed and released on the following Payout Date, in the composition set out in Section 15 (90% cash / 10% Gold Points).

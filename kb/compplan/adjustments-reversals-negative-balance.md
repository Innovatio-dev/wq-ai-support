# Adjustments, reversals and Negative Balance

## §16.1 Refunds and chargebacks

If a retail sale that generated BV is later refunded or charged back, the BV is removed from every calculation in which it was counted, for every period in which it was counted, regardless of the reason for the refund and regardless of the IBO's status at the time of the reversal.

Every payment calculated from that BV is reversed in the same measure:
- the Direct Bonus paid on it;
- the SITB accrued on any Rank it supported;
- the Generational Match paid on that SITB; and
- any Refer 4, Customer Launch or Fast 5 award for which it was a qualifying order.

## §16.2 Rank recalculation

Where reversed volume changes an IBO's Daily Rank Snapshot for one or more days, Rank is recalculated for those days and SITB is adjusted accordingly. Where the recalculation affects a Weekly Rank, the Generational Match for the affected period is recalculated as well.

## §16.3 Negative Balance

- Where reversals in a period exceed the amount otherwise payable to an IBO, the shortfall becomes a Negative Balance. It is carried forward and offset against the IBO's future compensation until cleared. It **does not accrue interest** and the Company **does not invoice** for it.
- It is offset against all subsequent compensation — Direct Bonus, SITB, Generational Match, the programs in Section 11, Leadership Rank Bonus installments and any Ambassador Travel Bonus award — until it is cleared.
- It does not stop compensation from accruing, and it does not remove Good Standing (§4.1). Stopping accrual would leave nothing to offset the balance against, which would make the balance permanent rather than self-clearing.

## §16.4 Notice

Adjustments and Negative Balances are shown in the Back Office with the underlying transaction identified, so that the reason for any change to a calculated amount can be traced to the specific order that produced it.

# Ambassador Travel Bonus

## §13.1 Purpose

An annual, performance-based travel award for the Plan's top-tier leaders, recognizing sustained Ambassador-level leadership across a full calendar year and funding a luxury travel experience for a leader and the team members they select. It is deliberately **not a personal reward**: the budget must be spent on a team experience.

## §13.2 Amount

A qualifying Ambassador earns a travel credit equal to **10% of their own total Stable Income Team Bonus earnings** accumulated during the Annual Qualification Period (1 January – 31 December). Issued as a dedicated travel credit, not as cash, and must be spent exclusively on qualifying team travel. Because the award is calculated from the leader's own SITB, it is self-funding: the organizational sales volume required to produce Ambassador-level SITB also produces the revenue that supports the award.

| Rank | Weekly SITB at Rank | Annual SITB (52 wks) | Travel credit (52 wks) | Annual SITB (26 wks) | Travel credit (26 wks) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R10 Bronze Ambassador | $29,995 | $1,559,740 | $155,974 | $779,870 | $77,987 |
| R11 Silver Ambassador | $39,900 | $2,074,800 | $207,480 | $1,037,400 | $103,740 |
| R12 Gold Ambassador | $50,050 | $2,602,600 | $260,260 | $1,301,300 | $130,130 |

> This table assumes uninterrupted Rank maintenance for the stated number of weeks, which is an assumption made to demonstrate the calculation and not an expectation. The 26-week columns are shown because a leader may qualify on as few as twelve weekly periods (§13.3), so a part-year outcome is at least as likely as a full-year one. The actual award is 10% of SITB actually earned. See Section 21.

## §13.3 Qualification

All of the following must be met in full:

1. **Rank.** Hold Bronze Ambassador (R10), Silver Ambassador (R11) or Gold Ambassador (R12) as the Weekly Rank for at least **twelve (12)** Weekly Commission Periods within the Annual Qualification Period. The twelve periods need not be consecutive.
2. **Qualified status.** Maintain Qualified IBO status and a current Monthly License Fee throughout the qualification period.
3. **Event attendance.** Attend the annual WealthQuest Leaders Event.
4. **Good Standing.** Be in Good Standing (§4.1) at the time of award.

**No partial qualification.** If any single criterion is not met, no award is made for that year. No pro-rating and no partial credit.

> The Ambassador Travel Bonus is a conditional annual award, not compensation earned on a transaction-by-transaction basis. No entitlement arises until every condition in §13.3 has been satisfied — including attendance at the Leaders Event. An IBO who does not attend has not qualified, rather than having qualified and then lost something. Section 15.5 of the IBO Agreement protects compensation already earned, and nothing in this Section overrides that protection in respect of Direct Bonus, SITB, Generational Match or any other earned compensation.

## §13.4 How the credit may be used

The credit funds luxury travel experiences for the leader and their selected team members. It may not be used for solo personal travel or for any non-team purpose.

| Scenario | Rule |
|---|---|
| Single trip | Maximum 12 people in total, including the leader. |
| Multiple trips | Permitted. The budget may be split across any number of trips. |
| Minimum group | Each trip must include at least 6 qualifying team members. |
| Solo use | Not permitted. The leader may not use the budget for personal travel alone. |

- **Qualifying uses:** private jet charter, private yacht charter, private villa or resort buyout, luxury hotel accommodation for the group, group excursions and activities during the trip, and ground transportation, meals and event costs tied to the travel experience.
- **Non-qualifying uses:** cash withdrawal or transfer of any kind; personal purchases unrelated to team travel; business expenses or product purchases; and carry-over to a following year — unused budget expires at the end of the payout window described in §13.5.
- **Eligible participants:** personally sponsored or downline IBOs within the leader's organization. Guests must be verified and approved before booking, and the leader is responsible for ensuring that all participants are legitimate team members.

## §13.5 Payout and processing

Calculated annually from total SITB earned between 1 January and 31 December and issued as a travel credit on the **first Friday of February** of the following calendar year, through the Company's approved travel booking platform or a verified reimbursement process.

**Formula: Travel credit = total annual SITB earnings × 10%.**

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Leader Rank | R12 Gold Ambassador |
| Weeks at qualifying Rank | 52 |
| Weekly SITB | $50,050 |
| Total annual SITB | $2,602,600 |
| Travel credit (10%) | $260,260 |

## §13.6 Company cap

Total Ambassador Travel Bonus awards across all qualifying leaders are subject to an internal Company cap. If aggregate awards would exceed the cap, each leader's award is pro-rated proportionally. The cap and the current threshold are published in the Back Office and may be changed only under Section 20.

## §13.7 Illustrations

- **Bronze Ambassador (R10), 52 qualifying weeks.** Annual SITB of approximately $1,559,740 produces a travel credit of approximately $156,000. That covers, for example, a private villa in Tuscany for 12 people over six nights with private jet transfers, private chef and guided excursions at an estimated $140,000 to $160,000; or two separate experiences — a Caribbean yacht charter for six team members over five days at approximately $75,000, and a ski chalet in Aspen for six team members over four nights at approximately $70,000.
- **Silver Ambassador (R11), 39 qualifying weeks.** Annual SITB of approximately $1,556,100 produces a travel credit of approximately $155,610 — enough for a private luxury yacht charter through the Greek Islands for 10 people over seven days, including round-trip flights, onboard chef, water sports and island excursions, at an estimated $140,000 to $160,000.
- **Gold Ambassador (R12), 52 qualifying weeks.** Annual SITB of approximately $2,602,600 produces a travel credit of approximately $260,260 — enough for a full private-island resort buyout in the Maldives for 12 people over seven nights with private jet transfers at an estimated $220,000 to $260,000; or three separate group experiences totalling approximately $250,000.
- **Bronze Ambassador (R10), 12 qualifying weeks — the Plan's minimum threshold.** Using the R10 Daily Accrual Amount ($4,285/day, or $29,995 for a 7-day week): 12 weeks × $29,995 Weekly SITB = **$359,940** Annual SITB; travel credit of 10% = **$35,994**. Enough, for example, for a single qualifying trip for 6 to 8 team members at a domestic resort over three or four nights, or a smaller allocation within the multi-trip structure in §13.4. This illustration sits at the low end: a qualifying Ambassador's actual award depends entirely on how many qualifying weeks are actually achieved, and the minimum twelve-week path is a realistic outcome, not only the 52- and 39-week paths. It assumes Rank maintenance for the twelve weeks stated and is not a projection of anyone's earnings.

These illustrations show how a stated budget could be spent. They assume Rank maintenance across the periods shown, are not projections of anyone's earnings, and the third-party travel costs quoted are indicative estimates, not prices offered or guaranteed by WealthQuest.

## §13.8 Verification, misuse and audit

The Company may request documentation confirming that the credit was used for qualifying team experiences, including receipts, booking confirmations and participant lists. Where credit is found to have been used for personal gain, converted to a cash equivalent, or applied to a non-qualifying purpose, the full amount may be reversed or deducted from future earnings, and the leader may be subject to compliance review and to exclusion from future Ambassador Travel Bonus eligibility. The Company may verify SITB accuracy, verify the count of qualifying weeks, audit travel usage, and verify the eligibility of participating team members.

## §13.9 Failure to qualify

No award is made for a year in which the leader fails to attend the annual WealthQuest Leaders Event, fails to hold a qualifying Ambassador Rank for at least twelve Weekly Commission Periods, is subject to an active compliance violation at the time of award, or has used a prior travel credit for a non-qualifying purpose.

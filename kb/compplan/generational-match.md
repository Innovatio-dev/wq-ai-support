# Generational Match

## §10.1 Purpose

Rewards a leader for developing other leaders. It pays a percentage of the SITB earned by qualifying leaders within the paying IBO's **Enroller Tree**, and therefore pays only to the extent that those leaders have themselves produced retail sales volume.

## §10.2 What the match is paid on

**Stable Income Team Bonus earnings only.** No match is paid on the Direct Bonus, the customer acquisition programs in Section 11, the Leadership Rank Bonus, the Ambassador Travel Bonus, Gold Points, or any non-BV transaction.

## §10.3 Rank used for the match

A Qualified IBO's Generational Match eligibility and percentage for a Weekly Commission Period are determined by their **Weekly Rank** — the highest Daily Rank Snapshot achieved during that period (§7.3). The underlying volume remains subject to the 31-day Volume Window in the ordinary way.

Worked example:

| Days | Daily Rank Snapshot |
|---|---|
| Tuesday – Friday | R4 Bronze Manager |
| Saturday – Monday | R3 Gold Associate |

This IBO is paid the Generational Match as a Rank 4 IBO for the whole of that week, because Rank 4 was the highest snapshot achieved.

## §10.4 Minimum Rank

A Weekly Rank of at least **Rank 4 (Bronze Manager)** is required. An IBO below Rank 4 for the whole of a period receives no Generational Match for that period.

## §10.5 What a Generation is

A Generation is the first IBO in a downline branch of your Enroller Tree who holds Rank 4 or above. Every Rank 4+ IBO starts a new Generation and separates their own downline from the Generation above them.

## §10.6 Rank 1 – Rank 3 roll-up

IBOs at Ranks 1 to 3 do not form a Generation. Their SITB earnings are aggregated and rolled up to the first Rank 4 or above IBO in the upline of the Enroller Tree, and are matched at that level. No SITB earnings are lost from the calculation; they are simply attributed upward until they reach a leader.

## §10.7 Match percentages

| Your Weekly Rank | Gen 1 | Gen 2 | Gen 3 | Gen 4 | Gen 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R4 – R5 | 5% | — | — | — | — |
| R6 – R7 | 5% | 5% | — | — | — |
| R8 – R9 | 5% | 5% | 4% | — | — |
| R10 – R12 | 5% | 5% | 4% | 3% | 2% |

A maximum of **five Generations** is paid, regardless of Rank or organizational depth.

## §10.8 Generation unlock requirements

Reaching the Rank in the table above makes a Generation available to you. You must separately unlock it by developing personally sponsored leaders, measured within the Volume Window:

| Generation | Personally sponsored leaders required (rolling 31 days) |
|---|---|
| Generation 1 | 2 personally sponsored at Rank 3 or above |
| Generation 2 | 3 personally sponsored at Rank 4 or above |
| Generation 3 | 4 personally sponsored at Rank 4 or above |
| Generation 4 | 5 personally sponsored at Rank 4 or above |
| Generation 5 | 6 personally sponsored at Rank 4 or above |

Both tests must be satisfied for a Generation to pay: your own Weekly Rank must make the Generation available (§10.7), and you must meet its unlock requirement. Unlock requirements are counted on personally sponsored IBOs in the Enroller Tree and are independent of whether a Generation at that level actually exists in your organization — if the level is available and unlocked but empty, compression applies (§10.9).

## §10.9 Compression

If a Generation level does not exist in your organization, the next qualifying Generation below it moves up to fill that level for payout purposes. If there is no Generation 2 but a Generation 3 exists, the Generation 3 leader is paid as your Generation 2.

## §10.10 How the weekly calculation is performed

1. **Determine Weekly Rank.** Select the highest Daily Rank Snapshot achieved during the period.
2. **Validate qualification.** Confirm Rank 4 or above, Qualified IBO status, Good Standing, and the applicable Generation unlock requirements.
3. **Build the Generation tree.** Traverse the Enroller Tree downward from the IBO, identifying the first Rank 4+ IBO in each branch as Generation 1, the next Rank 4+ below them as Generation 2, and so on.
4. **Apply the Rank 1 – Rank 3 roll-up.** Aggregate the SITB earnings of Rank 1 to Rank 3 IBOs into the nearest Rank 4+ upline in the Enroller Tree.
5. **Apply compression.** Move lower Generations up to fill any level that does not exist.
6. **Calculate.** Sum the SITB earnings attributed to each Generation, apply the percentage for the paying IBO's Weekly Rank, and total the result.

## §10.11 Qualification summary

To earn a Generational Match for a period you must hold Weekly Rank 4 or above, hold Qualified IBO status, be in Good Standing, and meet the unlock requirement for each Generation on which you are paid. If a requirement for a particular Generation is not met, that Generation does not pay; there is no partial payment at any level.

## §10.12 Adjustment and audit

Generational Match is calculated from SITB earnings and moves with them: where SITB is reversed for refunds, chargebacks or compliance action, the match paid on it is reversed in the same measure. The Company may verify Enroller Tree structure, audit Rank, and adjust payouts accordingly (§19).

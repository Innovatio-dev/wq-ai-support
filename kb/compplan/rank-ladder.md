# The Rank Ladder (R1–R12)

## §6.1 The twelve Ranks

Four Rank tiers of three levels each, numbered R1 through R12: **Associates** (Bronze, Silver, Gold), **Managers** (Bronze, Silver, Gold), **Executives** (Bronze, Silver, Gold) and **Ambassadors** (Bronze, Silver, Gold). Rank is earned by a Qualified IBO and is re-evaluated every day.

## §6.2 Rank qualification requirements

To hold a Rank on a given day, a Qualified IBO must meet both the weak-leg volume requirement and the structural requirement for that Rank, measured across the Volume Window ending on that day.

| Rank | Weak-leg BV (rolling 31 days) | Structure required |
|---|---|---|
| **Associates** | | |
| R1 Bronze Associate | 1,600 BV | 2 personally sponsored Active IBOs (1 left leg / 1 right leg) |
| R2 Silver Associate | 3,200 BV | 4 personally sponsored (2 left / 2 right) |
| R3 Gold Associate | 6,000 BV | 6 personally sponsored (3 left / 3 right) |
| **Managers** | | |
| R4 Bronze Manager | 12,000 BV | 8 personally sponsored, of whom 2 at Rank 2 or above |
| R5 Silver Manager | 24,000 BV | 10 personally sponsored, of whom 3 at Rank 2 or above |
| R6 Gold Manager | 48,000 BV | 12 personally sponsored, of whom 4 at Rank 3 or above |
| **Executives** | | |
| R7 Bronze Executive | 90,000 BV | 14 personally sponsored, of whom 2 at Rank 4 and 3 at Rank 3 |
| R8 Silver Executive | 150,000 BV | 16 personally sponsored, of whom 3 at Rank 4 and 3 at Rank 3 |
| R9 Gold Executive | 240,000 BV | 18 personally sponsored, and 2 Leadership Lines each containing a Rank 6 leader |
| **Ambassadors** | | |
| R10 Bronze Ambassador | 360,000 BV | 3 Leadership Lines each containing a Rank 6 leader |
| R11 Silver Ambassador | 480,000 BV | 2 Leadership Lines each containing a Rank 7 leader, and 2 further lines each containing a Rank 6 leader |
| R12 Gold Ambassador | 600,000 BV | 3 Leadership Lines each containing a Rank 7 leader, and 2 further lines each containing a Rank 6 leader |

**How many distinct lines the Ambassador Ranks require.** Because a line counted as containing a Rank 7 leader may not also be counted as one of the lines containing a Rank 6 leader (§6.3), and because every lower Rank's structure must also be satisfied on the same day (§6.4), **Rank 11 requires five distinct qualifying Leadership Lines and Rank 12 requires six.**

## §6.3 How the structural requirements are counted

- "Personally sponsored" means enrolled directly under your Sponsor ID, counted in the Enroller Tree. A personally sponsored IBO counts toward your requirement only on a day on which that IBO themselves holds Active IBO status.
- Left / right allocation at Ranks 1 to 3 is determined by the leg of your Placement Tree in which the personally sponsored IBO sits.
- Rank conditions on personally sponsored IBOs ("2 at Rank 2 or above") are measured on that IBO's Daily Rank Snapshot for the same day.
- Leadership Lines are counted in the Enroller Tree (§4.3). A leader at the required Rank may sit at any depth within the line. One line may not be counted twice, and a line counted as containing a Rank 7 leader may not simultaneously be counted as one of the lines containing a Rank 6 leader.

## §6.4 No partial qualification

- A Qualified IBO must meet every requirement for a Rank to hold that Rank. There is no prorated or partial Rank, and no partial earnings at a Rank whose requirements are only partly met. An IBO who meets the requirements for Rank 5 but not Rank 6 holds Rank 5 and is paid at Rank 5.
- **No skipping Ranks.** To hold a given Rank, a Qualified IBO must also satisfy the weak-leg volume and structural requirements of every Rank below it in the table, measured on the same day. A Rank is not held merely because its own row is satisfied in isolation. In practice this matters most for the left/right balance stated explicitly only for Ranks 1 to 3: an IBO may not hold Rank 4 or above on the strength of a headcount and Rank-composition requirement alone while leaving one leg of the Placement Tree without the personally sponsored IBOs Ranks 1 through 3 would have required.

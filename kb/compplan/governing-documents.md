# Governing documents and order of precedence

## §2.1 The documents

Your relationship with WealthQuest is governed by six documents, each incorporated into the others by reference. Together they form the whole of your agreement with the Company — this Plan is one part of it and does not stand alone.

| Document | What it governs | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| IBO Agreement — General Part, with Schedule C | The contract between you and WealthQuest: enrollment, status, activation and eligibility, termination, liability, intellectual property, and the governing law and dispute resolution that apply to your relationship with the Company under Schedule C, which names WealthQuest, LLC as the Company's sole Contracting Entity. | Back Office, and https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/ibo-agreement |
| Product Appendixes | Product-specific rules and benefits, one per product line. Each must be separately and affirmatively accepted before you are eligible for compensation on that line. | Back Office |
| Policies and Procedures | The operational rulebook for day-to-day conduct: ethical dealing, how you may market and advertise, prohibited claims, trademark use, sponsorship conduct, order and returns handling, and cooperation with investigations. | https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/policies-procedures |
| Sales Compensation Plan (this document) | How compensation is calculated and paid: status and rank, every income stream, payout timing and composition, adjustments, and plan integrity. | Back Office, and https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/compensation-plan |
| Income Disclosure Statement | The Company's authoritative source for earnings information, and the only permitted basis for any earnings claim by the Company or by an IBO. | https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/income-disclosure |
| Privacy Policy | How personal data is collected, used, shared and retained. | https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/privacy-policy |

The Policies and Procedures and the Income Disclosure Statement are published as standalone documents. Both are binding in the same way this Plan is. Where this Plan touches on a subject one of them governs — how you may talk about earnings, for example — it points you there rather than restating the rule, so that there is only ever one authoritative version of it.

## §2.2 Order of precedence

- **Plan vs. IBO Agreement:** the Agreement controls on questions of participant status, activation and eligibility; this Plan controls on questions of how a payout is calculated and in what amount.
- **Plan vs. Policies and Procedures:** the Agreement controls.
- **Product Appendix:** a product-specific rule applies to that product line only and may not create a separate status ladder, activation process or qualification track.
- **Income Disclosure Statement:** does not create, alter or limit any compensation right. It governs what may be said about earnings; this Plan governs what is earned. On a question of calculation or amount, this Plan controls; on any question of what may be represented to a Customer or prospective IBO about income, the Income Disclosure Statement and Section 4.2 of the Policies and Procedures control.
- Terms defined in the IBO Agreement carry the same meaning in this Plan. Where this Plan defines a term the Agreement does not, the definition in Section 4 applies.

## §2.3 Product Appendix eligibility

- You are eligible for compensation on a product line's sales only once you have affirmatively accepted the applicable Product Appendix in your Back Office (Agreement, Section 9). Until you do, sales of that product line through your link generate no BV and no compensation for you, and you may not market or promote that product line at all.
- **At launch, WealthVentures is this Plan's only live product line**, and no other product line generates BV or compensation under this Plan.
- Where GoldMind or Liquid Gold are named in this Plan, they are named only to distinguish them from Gold Points.
- As additional product lines are ready to launch or enter presale, each will be added to this Plan under its own accepted Product Appendix. Until a line is added on that basis, it generates no BV or compensation of any kind, and no product-line-specific mechanic in this Plan (the Refer 4 program, §11.1; the Fast 5 Bonus, §11.3; the Gold Points redemption limits, §15.4) applies to it.

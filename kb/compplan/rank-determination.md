# Rank determination: daily snapshot, Weekly Rank, volume expiry

## §7.1 Daily calculation

Rank is recalculated at **12:00 a.m. Pacific Time every day**. The system evaluates the preceding 31 calendar days and assigns the IBO a **Daily Rank Snapshot** for that day. That snapshot governs what the IBO accrues under the Stable Income Team Bonus for that day. This daily re-evaluation is Rank Maintenance (§4.4): the volume and structural conditions are measured as trailing 31-day totals ending on the day of calculation, not as an average and not as a sum across the days of a week.

## §7.2 Rank can fall as well as rise

Rank reflects current sustained performance, not past achievement. It may increase or decrease on any day, and there is **no floor**: an IBO who held Rank 8 last month and whose organization has produced no volume in the last 31 days holds no Rank today. The only awards that reference past achievement are the Leadership Rank Bonus and the Ambassador Travel Bonus, which use the Sustained Rank Standard (§4.4).

## §7.3 Weekly Rank

The Weekly Rank is the highest Daily Rank Snapshot achieved during the Weekly Commission Period. It determines eligibility and percentage under the Generational Match (§10.3), and it is the measure by which a Rank counts as achieved in a week for the Sustained Rank Standard used by the Leadership Rank Bonus (§12.2) and the Ambassador Travel Bonus (§13.3). **SITB is not calculated from Weekly Rank**; it accrues on each day's own snapshot.

Summary table (derived from §7.1, §7.3, §12.2 and §13.3):

| Used for | Rank measure |
|---|---|
| SITB | Each day's Daily Rank Snapshot |
| Generational Match | Weekly Rank |
| Leadership Rank Bonus | Weekly Rank in 4 of the last 8 periods |
| Ambassador Travel Bonus | Weekly Rank in 12 periods within the calendar year |

## §7.4 Volume expiry

BV counts toward Rank for as long as it remains within the Volume Window. It enters on the day the sale completes and leaves on the **thirty-second day**. Volume that has left the window cannot be restored, carried forward or reinstated. Strong Leg Volume is tracked and remains valid only while inside its own rolling 31-day Volume Window (§4.2), and expires with that volume.

## §7.5 Verification

All Rank qualifications are provisional until verified. The Company may verify volume, customer legitimacy, placement and the status of personally sponsored IBOs at any time, and may adjust a Rank and any compensation paid on the basis of it (§19).

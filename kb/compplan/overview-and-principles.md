# Overview and principles

> Earning income as a WealthQuest Independent Business Owner is not typical, is not guaranteed, and requires meaningful personal effort. WealthQuest is a newly launched company and has no earnings history of any kind. Every figure in this document illustrates how the Plan's arithmetic works; none of it is a projection of what anyone will earn. Read Section 21 and the WealthQuest Income Disclosure Statement before enrolling.

The WealthQuest Compensation Plan Agreement (effective 10.08.2026, WealthQuest, LLC) forms part of the WealthQuest Independent Business Owner Agreement.

## §1.1 Purpose

This Sales Compensation Plan (the "Plan") sets out how WealthQuest calculates and pays compensation to its Independent Business Owners ("IBOs"). It is written to be read in full before you enroll, and to be relied on afterwards. Every amount, threshold, period and condition that determines what you are paid appears in this document.

The Plan is one of six documents that together govern your WealthQuest business (see [[governing-documents]]).

## §1.2 The principle the Plan is built on

- All compensation under this Plan derives from the sale of WealthQuest products and services to genuine end Customers.
- No compensation of any kind — no commission, bonus, credit, award or benefit — is paid for enrolling, sponsoring or recruiting another participant.
- Sponsoring another IBO can increase what you earn, but only to the extent that the people you sponsor go on to make real retail sales to real Customers. If they make no sales, you earn nothing from having sponsored them. This is how every calculation in the Plan is constructed (Sections 8 through 13).
- Neither the Activation Fee nor the Monthly License Fee is commissionable. Neither generates Business Volume. Neither counts toward any Rank. A payment made by an IBO to WealthQuest never produces compensation for anyone, at any level, in any income stream.

## §1.3 What this Plan does not do

This Plan does not guarantee income. It describes a method of calculation, not a result. Every figure — every table, example and illustration — shows how the arithmetic works if the stated conditions are met. None of it is a forecast, a projection, or a statement about what any person has earned or is likely to earn. See [[earnings-disclaimer]].

## §1.4 Retail Sales Basis

- The Company monitors, on an ongoing, company-wide basis, the ratio of aggregate compensation attributable to verified retail sales to Verified/Independent Customers against aggregate compensation attributable to downline or structural bonuses (Stable Income Team Bonus, Generational Match, the Leadership Rank Bonus and the Ambassador Travel Bonus).
- The ratio is measured at least quarterly. The target is that a substantial majority — **not less than sixty percent (60%)** — of aggregate compensation paid in each measurement period derives from compensation attributable to verified retail sales to Verified/Independent Customers.
- Where a measurement period falls below that target, the Company will adjust this Plan, consistent with the amendment procedure in Section 20, so that the substantial majority of aggregate compensation derives from bona fide retail sales rather than from recruitment or tree placement.
- This is a company-wide, aggregate commitment; it does not limit, and is not applied against, any individual IBO's own compensation.

## §3 How WealthQuest Pays — the eight income streams

Three are paid in money every week, one is issued as an activation code, two are paid on verification, one is paid monthly, and one is an annual team travel award. This table is a map, not a substitute for Part IV.

| Income stream | What it pays on | Who is eligible | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Bonus | 10% of BV on your own verified retail sales | Active IBO | Weekly |
| Stable Income Team Bonus (SITB) | A fixed daily amount set by your Rank, which is itself set by rolling 31-day weak-leg BV and structure | Qualified IBO at Rank 1+ | Accrues daily, paid weekly |
| Generational Match | A percentage of the SITB earned by leaders in your Enroller Tree | Qualified IBO at Rank 4+ | Weekly |
| Refer 4, Pay No More | Four personally referred Customers on the same product you hold | Active IBO | Evaluated weekly |
| Customer Launch Bonus | Retail volume produced by a newly sponsored IBO's Customers in that IBO's first 31 days | Active IBO | On verification |
| Fast 5 Bonus | Five unique Customers whose first purchase alone reaches 500 BV, of any product line | Active IBO | On verification |
| Leadership Rank Bonus | Sustained achievement of Rank 5 or above | Qualified IBO at Rank 5+ | Monthly, up to 12 installments |
| Ambassador Travel Bonus | 10% of your annual SITB, issued as team travel credit | Qualified IBO at Rank 10–12 | Annual |

Every income stream requires a current Monthly License Fee and at least Active IBO status. The four rank-based streams — SITB, Generational Match, Leadership Rank Bonus and Ambassador Travel Bonus — additionally require Qualified IBO status and Good Standing. None of them pays on enrollment, sponsorship or recruitment.

## §3.1 Aggregate Payout Cap

- Aggregate compensation calculated for a Weekly Commission Period will not exceed **sixty percent (60%)** of the Company's revenue recognized for that period. The cap applies to the sum of all income streams across all IBOs for the period; it does not limit, and is not applied against, any individual IBO's own compensation.
- **Revenue base:** WealthQuest, LLC's recognised revenue for the period from WealthVentures membership Activation and Subscription payments. WealthQuest, LLC receives no booking revenue, margin or commission from any third-party travel supplier, and no such amount forms part of the revenue base.
- If aggregate compensation would exceed the cap, the Company may suspend release of payouts until the following Weekly Commission Period. A suspension is a **hold, not a forfeiture**: compensation already calculated continues to be treated as Eligible Earnings and is released once the cap condition is satisfied, on the same footing as compensation held during a suspension under Section 19.3. This Section creates no right to reduce, cancel or claw back compensation already released to an IBO's Wallet.
- **Release sequencing:** the excess is held and released in the next Weekly Commission Period in which headroom under the cap exists. Held amounts are released oldest-accrued first, and before that period's own accruals. Held amounts are shown in the Back Office, so that what is held, and from which period, can be seen at any time.

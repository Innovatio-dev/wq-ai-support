# Participant status levels and fees

Status Level (Applicant, Enrolled — Pending Activation, Enrolled — Paused Activation, Active, Qualified) describes an IBO's enrollment/activity standing; Rank (R1–R12, Section 6) describes an IBO's position on the compensation ladder. Status Level and Rank are tracked and reported separately.

## §5.1 The ladder

| Status | License fee | What it earns under this Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | None | Nothing. A Customer is not a participant and has no rights under this Plan. |
| Brand Advocate | None | Nothing. May share a referral link as personal enthusiasm only; may not sponsor and may not make earnings claims. |
| Applicant | Activation Fee and first Monthly License Fee paid; further billing suspended | Nothing. Holds no Rank and accrues no BV. The placement position is reserved but not active. Further Monthly License Fee billing is suspended while Applicant status is held and resumes on verification. |
| Enrolled — Pending Activation | $99 + $29/mo | Nothing yet. May access training and tools. May not sponsor. The first verified retail sale confers Active IBO status and is itself commissionable (§8.2). |
| Enrolled — Paused Activation | Lapsed | Nothing while paused. Account, tree position, sales history and Rank preserved. |
| Active IBO | $99 + $29/mo | Direct Bonus (§8) and the customer acquisition programs (§11). |
| Qualified IBO | $99 + $29/mo | All of the above, plus SITB (§9), Generational Match (§10), Leadership Rank Bonus (§12) and Ambassador Travel Bonus (§13), each subject to its own Rank requirements. |

**What the fees include.**
- The **$99 Activation Fee** covers the Welcome onboarding video, setup and connection of all WealthQuest apps to your account, and the Exclusive Leadership Video, made available in the Back Office shortly after activation.
- The **$29 Monthly License Fee** covers that month's access to WealthQuest tools, the invoicing system, the binary system and genealogy tree overview, and that month's allotment of tool tokens.
- The Monthly License Fee renews automatically each month until cancelled, and may be cancelled at any time through a process no more difficult than enrollment, effective for the next billing cycle.
- Every new IBO has **fourteen (14) days** from enrollment to cancel for any reason and receive a full refund. Full terms are in Section 6 of the IBO Agreement.
- Neither fee is commissionable, generates BV, or counts toward any Rank.

## §5.2 No earnings below Active IBO

No status below Active IBO earns any commission, bonus, credit or other compensation of any kind. Paying the Activation Fee and the Monthly License Fee does not, by itself, make anyone eligible for anything. The first verified retail sale to a Verified/Independent Customer — not enrollment, not a personal purchase, not sponsoring anyone — is what turns compensation on, and that first sale is itself commissionable (§8.2).

An Enrolled — Pending Activation participant may also not sponsor or recruit anyone (Agreement, Section 4.3). Sponsoring becomes available at Active IBO status, on the same first sale that turns compensation on — so a single verified retail sale unlocks both at once.

## §5.3 How participants are reported

Every person who has paid the Activation Fee is reported as a participant, at every status level, in all Company reporting and in the Income Disclosure Statement, whether or not they have produced a sale or achieved any Rank. This includes Applicants, including Applicants whose verification did not clear and whose applications were declined. Participants who have never earned are reported, and the percentage of participants earning nothing is disclosed. Customers are reported separately and are never counted as participants. No participant population is reclassified as Customers for any purpose.

## §5.4 Status is continuous, not permanent

- Active and Qualified status are determined continuously. Both turn off automatically for as long as the Monthly License Fee lapses, and turn back on automatically, with no re-qualification step, as soon as it is current again. The sales conditions already satisfied are never erased by a lapse (Agreement, Section 6).
- **Grace period:** a failed or missed payment does not take effect as a lapse immediately. It triggers the **twenty-four (24) hour** grace period in Section 6 of the IBO Agreement, during which you retain full Active and Qualified status and can cure the payment. A sale made during the grace period generates BV in the ordinary way. The lapse takes effect only at the end of that period if the payment has not been cured, and the grace period does not apply to your own affirmative cancellation.
- **Reactivation** restores status immediately, but not payout at full strength. Because Rank is calculated from a rolling 31-day total and no BV was generated during a lapse, the rolling figure remains suppressed for a period after reactivation even with strong new sales. This is the ordinary mechanical effect of the same formula applied to every IBO at all times, not a penalty.

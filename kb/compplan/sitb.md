# Stable Income Team Bonus (SITB)

## §9.1 Purpose

The Plan's principal team income stream. It pays a Qualified IBO a fixed amount for each day on which they hold a Rank, where Rank is itself determined by rolling 31-day weak-leg volume and organizational structure. The design objective is stability: because Rank is measured on a 31-day rolling basis rather than on a single week's sales, a strong week does not spike earnings and a quiet week does not collapse them.

## §9.2 Daily accrual, weekly payment

SITB accrues daily and is paid weekly. Each day, the Daily Rank Snapshot (§7.1) determines a Daily Accrual Amount, credited to the IBO's ledger for that day. The sum of the seven Daily Accrual Amounts in a Weekly Commission Period is released to the Wallet on the following Payout Date.

> Nothing under this Plan is paid out daily. "Daily" describes how the amount is calculated and accrued, not when money moves. All money moves weekly, on the Payout Date (§14.2).

The 7-day and 30-day columns are arithmetic only — the daily figure multiplied by 7 and by 30. They show what would accrue if the same Rank were held on every day of the period, which is an assumption made to demonstrate the calculation. They are not projections, are not typical, and no IBO should assume any Rank will be held continuously. See Section 21.

## §9.4 Weekly calculation

**Weekly SITB = the sum of the Daily Accrual Amounts for each of the seven days in the Weekly Commission Period.** Because Rank is recalculated daily, the seven amounts need not be equal.

## §9.5 Worked example

An IBO whose Rank moves during the week accrues at whatever Rank applied on each individual day:

| Day | Daily Rank Snapshot | Daily accrual |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | R4 Bronze Manager | $140 |
| Wednesday | R4 Bronze Manager | $140 |
| Thursday | R5 Silver Manager | $285 |
| Friday | R5 Silver Manager | $285 |
| Saturday | R6 Gold Manager | $570 |
| Sunday | R6 Gold Manager | $570 |
| Monday | R6 Gold Manager | $570 |
| **Total for the period** | | **$2,560** |

The $2,560 is released on the Friday following the Monday close, in the composition set out in Section 15. This is an illustration of the arithmetic, not a representation of typical earnings.

## §9.6 Eligibility

To accrue SITB on a given day, an IBO must, on that day:

1. hold Qualified IBO status (§4.1);
2. have a current Monthly License Fee;
3. be in Good Standing (§4.1);
4. have at least two personally sponsored Active IBOs, at least one in the left leg and at least one in the right leg of the Placement Tree; and
5. meet the weak-leg volume and structural requirements of at least Rank 1 (§6.2).

An IBO who satisfies conditions 1 to 4 but not condition 5 is a **Qualified IBO — Unranked** for that day and accrues no SITB for that day. Nothing else about their status changes.

## §9.7 Weak leg and strong leg

- Rank qualification is based on Weak Leg Volume over the Volume Window (§4.2).
- The Weak Leg is the leg with the lesser accumulated BV; leg designation is determined separately each day.
- Strong Leg Volume is tracked and remains valid only while inside its own rolling 31-day Volume Window (§4.2), and expires with that volume.
- Volume remains valid only while inside the Volume Window and expires on the thirty-second day.

## §9.8 No partial qualification

An IBO must meet every requirement of a Rank to accrue at that Rank. There is no prorated accrual and no partial-Rank accrual.

## §9.9 Integrity and adjustment

All SITB accrual is provisional until verified. Accrued or paid SITB may be withheld, reversed or adjusted where the underlying volume is reversed by refund or chargeback, where Rank was achieved through manipulation of volume, placement or customer records, or where the IBO is found not to have been in Good Standing on the relevant day. See Sections 16 and 19.

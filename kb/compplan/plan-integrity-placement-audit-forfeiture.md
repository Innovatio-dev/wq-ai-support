# Plan integrity: account rules, placement, audit and forfeiture

## §17 Account Integrity and Verification

**§17.1 One account per person.** No individual may hold more than one active IBO account. Enrollment is tied to a unique verified identity, and a person who has completed KYC for one account may not complete KYC for a second (Agreement, Section 3).

**§17.2 Unique mobile number.** Every account must complete SMS verification at enrollment against a mobile telephone number that is unique to that account. A number already verified on another account cannot be used to verify a new one. Enrollment cannot be completed without successful SMS verification.

**§17.3 Unique KYC record.** Every account must hold a unique verified KYC record before any funds may be moved out of it (§14.4). A KYC identity already verified on another account will not verify a second account, and funds in an account that cannot be uniquely verified are not released.

**§17.4 Prohibited conduct.** The following are material breaches of this Plan and of the IBO Agreement:
- holding or controlling more than one IBO account, whether directly or through another person;
- enrolling in the name of another person, a fictitious person, or a person who has not genuinely consented;
- creating, or causing the creation of, fabricated, duplicated or nominee Customer accounts;
- placing orders for the purpose of manufacturing a qualification, Rank, or bonus trigger rather than for genuine end use;
- manipulating BV, Rank, customer counts or placement, including by directing volume to a leg for the purpose of producing weak-leg credit without corresponding retail sales; and
- any other artificial activity designed to produce compensation not supported by genuine retail sales.

**§17.5 Consequences.** Conduct under §17.4 may result in reversal of the affected compensation, forfeiture under §19.4, suspension pending investigation under Section 15.9 of the IBO Agreement, and termination for cause under Section 15.3 of the Agreement.

## §18 Placement Rules

**§18.1 Placement at enrollment.** Every IBO is assigned exactly one position in the global Placement Tree at enrollment, based on their Sponsor and the placement rules of this Plan. Positions may not be bought, sold, transferred, gifted or exchanged, and an IBO may not hold or control more than one position.

**§18.2 Inside-leg placement is prohibited.** A Sponsor may not place a personally sponsored IBO on an Inside Leg position (§4.3).
- The restriction applies at every level of the Placement Tree, not solely to the individual who personally enrolls a new participant: whoever is doing the placing may place a personally sponsored IBO only on the **Outside Leg** of the team member receiving that placement, never on that team member's Inside Leg.
- In practice, an IBO builds their organization by placing new enrollees on their team members' outside legs — never directly beneath a team member on that team member's own inside leg, regardless of how far down the tree that team member sits.
- The restriction prevents volume from being concentrated in positions that generate weak-leg credit without corresponding independently built organization. It is enforced by the system at the point of placement: a placement that would breach it is **rejected rather than reversed** after the fact.
- An attempt to circumvent this restriction — including by routing an enrollment through another account to achieve a placement that would otherwise be rejected — is prohibited conduct under §17.4.

**§18.3 Termination and tree position.** On termination, an IBO's position is not reassigned, transferred or inherited by any other IBO. The structure of the tree stays intact and the position simply becomes, and remains, empty: it generates no further BV, and no IBO is compensated on account of it (Agreement, Section 15.4).

## §19 Audit, Adjustment and Forfeiture

**§19.1 Audit rights.** The Company may audit any qualification, Rank, placement, customer record or payment at any time, including by contacting a Customer directly to verify that a sale was genuine and that the product was purchased for end use.

**§19.2 Payments are provisional until verified.** All calculated compensation is provisional until the underlying volume and qualifications have been verified and the applicable refund and chargeback windows have passed. Release to the Wallet does not waive the Company's right to adjust under Section 16.

**§19.3 Suspension pending investigation.** Where the Company reasonably suspects a breach, it may suspend Back Office access, compensation eligibility, or both, for a reasonable period while it investigates.
- Suspension is not termination: account, KYC record, tree position, sales history and Rank remain intact.
- Compensation continues to accrue during a suspension and is **held, not forfeited**. An open investigation does not remove Good Standing (§4.1) and does not stop accrual at source.
- Held amounts are released in full if no violation is found, and are dealt with under §19.4 only if a violation is established and results in termination for cause.
- The Company will reach an outcome and notify the IBO within the period set by Section 15.9 of the IBO Agreement.

**§19.4 Forfeiture — the closed list.** Compensation may be forfeited only on one of the following grounds, and on no other:
1. reversal of the underlying sale by refund or chargeback (§16.1);
2. a finding, following investigation, that the qualification on which the compensation was calculated, or the compensation itself, was produced by conduct prohibited under §17.4, where that conduct resulted in termination for cause under Section 15.3 of the IBO Agreement — absent termination, compensation calculated on such a qualification is reversed under Section 16 rather than forfeited;
3. failure to satisfy a stated condition of a conditional award before it becomes payable — specifically, an unmet installment condition under §12.4 or an unmet qualification condition under §13.3; and
4. misuse of an Ambassador Travel Bonus credit under §13.8.

- **Forfeiture is causation-based.** Termination for cause does not, by itself, forfeit compensation. Compensation already earned on a bona fide, already-completed retail sale to a Verified/Independent Customer — a Direct Bonus, or SITB or Generational Match accrued on volume from that sale — is not forfeited, even where the IBO is terminated for cause, unless Ground 2 is established in respect of that compensation. Forfeiture under Ground 2 reaches only compensation that the prohibited conduct actually produced.
- **Burden of proof.** The burden of establishing a ground of forfeiture, and the causal link between the prohibited conduct and the compensation said to be forfeited, rests with the Company.
- No compensation is forfeited because a Monthly License Fee lapses, because an IBO cancels, because an IBO does not renew, or on the basis of any subjective assessment of an IBO's conduct, attitude or alignment with Company values. Compensation Gold Points (§15.2) are never forfeited.

**§19.5 Errors.** Where the Company identifies a calculation error in its own favour, it corrects it and pays the difference on the next Payout Date without requiring a request from the IBO. Where an error operated in the IBO's favour, the Company recovers the overpayment under §16.3.

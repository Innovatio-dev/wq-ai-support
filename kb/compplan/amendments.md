# Amendments to the Plan

This Plan may be amended only in accordance with Section 19 of the IBO Agreement. In particular:

- A change to a bonus percentage, a Rank threshold, a Daily Accrual Amount, a leg or placement rule, a BV assignment, or any other payout mechanic requires **at least 31 days' advance notice**, takes effect only at the start of a new Weekly Commission Period and **never mid-period**, and does not retroactively reduce compensation already accrued as of the notice date (Agreement, Section 19.2).
- Because BV attaches to the product rather than to price (§4.2), a change to a product's BV assignment is a change to this Plan and follows the notice requirements above, whether or not the product's price also changes.
- An ordinary price change made for genuine commercial reasons is not, by itself, an amendment to this Plan, because under §4.2 a price change does not alter the product's BV. Where a price change is accompanied by a change to that product's BV assignment, the BV change follows the notice requirements above regardless of the commercial reason for the price change (Agreement, Section 19.3).
- An IBO who does not wish to continue under changed terms may terminate voluntarily before the effective date without forfeiting compensation already earned (Agreement, Sections 15.2 and 15.5).

Cross-references (from §4.2, §12.3 and §13.6): items that may change without the §20 notice, because they alter no percentage, threshold or amount: the Vehicle Branding Guidelines (§12.3). Items that may be changed only under §20: the Product & BV Schedule (§4.2) and the Ambassador Travel Bonus Company cap and threshold (§13.6).

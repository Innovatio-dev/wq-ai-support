# Structure: Placement Tree, Enroller Tree, Leadership Lines, Inside Leg

## Placement Tree (Binary Tree)

The single global binary structure in which every IBO occupies exactly one position with two legs beneath it, a **Left Leg** and a **Right Leg**.
- Team volume accumulates in the Placement Tree, and the Placement Tree **alone** determines Weak Leg Volume, Strong Leg Volume and Rank.
- There is one Placement Tree, spanning all product lines, under WealthQuest, LLC as the Company's sole Contracting Entity today; if and when the Company forms additional Contracting Entities, the Placement Tree will remain a single structure spanning all of them.

## Enroller Tree

The separate structure recording who personally sponsored whom. The Enroller Tree **alone** determines Generational Match, personally sponsored counts, Leadership Lines and the customer acquisition programs in Section 11.

## Leadership Line

The downline organization tracing from one personally sponsored IBO in the Enroller Tree, together with everyone that IBO has sponsored, directly or indirectly. Where a Rank requirement in Section 6 refers to "lines containing" a leader at a given Rank, it refers to distinct Leadership Lines in the Enroller Tree — **not** to the two legs of the Placement Tree, of which every IBO has exactly two.

## Inside Leg / Outside Leg

For any given IBO in the Placement Tree, the **Inside Leg** is the leg of that IBO's own position that lies on the same side as, and directly beneath, that IBO's position relative to their own upline — the leg into which volume would flow upward to that IBO's Sponsor without passing through an independently built organization. The other leg is the **Outside Leg**.

- Inside Leg is measured from the perspective of whichever IBO is actually receiving a new placement, at every level of the Placement Tree — not solely from the perspective of the top-level Sponsor.
- Placement of a personally sponsored IBO on the Inside Leg of the team member who is actually placing them is prohibited under §18.2 (see [[plan-integrity-placement-audit-forfeiture]]).

## Territory

The country or region in which an IBO is resident and for which a Contracting Entity is designated under Schedule C of the IBO Agreement.

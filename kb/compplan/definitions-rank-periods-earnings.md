# Definitions: rank, periods, earnings, payment and compliance

## §4.4 Rank and periods

**Rank.** One of the twelve levels in the ladder set out in Section 6, achieved by meeting the weak-leg volume and structural requirements for that level within the Volume Window.

**Daily Rank Snapshot.** The Rank assigned to an IBO for a given day, calculated at **12:00 a.m. Pacific Time** each day by evaluating the preceding 31 days.

**Weekly Rank.** The highest Daily Rank Snapshot achieved during a Weekly Commission Period. A Rank is "achieved in" a Weekly Commission Period, wherever this Plan uses that phrase, if it is the Weekly Rank for that period. Weekly Rank determines Generational Match eligibility and percentage (§10.3) and is the measure used by the Sustained Rank Standard. It is **not** used for SITB, which accrues on each day's own snapshot.

**Rank Maintenance.** The requirement that the volume and structural conditions for a Rank continue to be met, measured afresh on each day of the Volume Window. A Rank is not permanently held: it is re-earned daily, and it can fall as well as rise. Rank Maintenance determines the amount payable to a Qualified IBO at a given Rank in a given period, and it is the standard the IBO Agreement refers to by that name.

**Sustained Rank Standard.** A separate, longer-horizon test used by the two long-cycle awards: achievement of the relevant Rank as the Weekly Rank in **four (4) of the last eight (8)** Weekly Commission Periods for the Leadership Rank Bonus (§12), and in **twelve (12)** Weekly Commission Periods within a calendar year for the Ambassador Travel Bonus (§13).

**Weekly Commission Period.** Begins **Tuesday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time** and ends the following **Monday at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time**.

**Payout Date.** **Friday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time** following the close of a Weekly Commission Period, on which calculated amounts for that period are released to the IBO's Wallet.

**Annual Qualification Period.** The calendar year, 1 January to 31 December, used solely to determine eligibility for the Ambassador Travel Bonus.

## §4.5 Earnings and payment

**Commission.** Any compensation paid to an IBO under this Plan on the basis of product sales or organizational sales performance.

**Stable Income Team Bonus ("SITB").** The rank-based team bonus defined in Section 9, which accrues a fixed amount for each day on which a Qualified IBO holds a Rank and is paid weekly.

**Commissionable Event.** A completed, paid and verified transaction that generates BV and qualifies for payout under this Plan.

**Wallet.** The internal ledger balance WealthQuest maintains on an IBO's Back Office account to track compensation owed before payout. It tracks three balances: the **cash-payable** balance, the **Compensation Gold Points** balance and the **Reward Gold Points** balance. The cash and Gold Points components of calculated compensation are credited to the Wallet on the Payout Date, and an IBO may withdraw the cash component to the Payout Provider, subject to verification (§14.4). The Wallet is not a stored-value account, a deposit account, an e-money instrument, or a cryptocurrency wallet, and it does not hold customer or IBO funds in custody outside the ordinary payment-processing cycle.

**Payout Provider.** PayQuicker, the Company's designated global payout provider, through which withdrawals of the cash component are executed for every IBO worldwide. If and when the Company forms additional Contracting Entities, PayQuicker will continue to serve every IBO on the same basis. The Company may designate an additional or replacement Payout Provider on reasonable advance notice (Agreement, Section 19.1).

**Gold Points ("GP").** A non-cash internal reward credit, where one Gold Point represents one US dollar of redemption value against WealthQuest products, subscriptions, upgrades and events — like an airline mileage award. Issued in two classes:
- **Compensation Gold Points** — the 10% Gold Points share of IBO compensation (§15.2). Earned compensation. They never expire and are never forfeited, including on lapse, cancellation, non-renewal or suspension.
- **Reward Gold Points** — issued with a WealthVentures membership Activation or monthly Subscription payment, and promotional awards including Refer 4 (§15.3). On account lapse — cancellation, non-payment or suspension — held for **ninety (90) days**, restored in full on reactivation within that window, and forfeited on the 90th day if the account is not reactivated. A Refer 4 award keeps its separate **forty (40) day** life as an activation code (§11.1).

Both classes: no cash value; not money, a security, a stored-value instrument, a deposit or a claim against the Company; never redeemable for cash or convertible into any currency or cryptocurrency; not transferable, giftable, saleable or assignable, or movable between accounts, under any circumstance — including on termination, account transfer, or death; redeemable only for what WealthQuest makes available (§15.4); cannot be applied in part; generate no BV and no commission when used; subject to the redemption limits in §15.4. Gold Points are live now. They are entirely distinct from **GoldMind** and **Liquid Gold**, which are unrelated to Gold Points, are not cryptocurrency, digital-asset, or precious-metal-backed products, and have no Product Appendix, are not available for enrollment, and generate no compensation until each separately completes its own compliance review and goes live. Gold Points are not linked to the market price of gold, any other commodity, or any cryptocurrency.

**Negative Balance.** The amount by which reversals applied to an IBO's account (refunds, chargebacks, adjustments and clawbacks) exceed the amount otherwise payable at the close of a Weekly Commission Period. Carried forward and offset against future compensation until cleared. It does not accrue interest, and the Company does not invoice for it.

**Eligible Earnings.** Compensation that has passed verification, is not subject to an unresolved reversal, and was earned at a time when the IBO held the status required for that income stream. Eligible Earnings are what is released to the Wallet on the Payout Date (§14.2). Whether an IBO still holds that status on the Payout Date does not affect Eligible Earnings already accrued (§8.6).

## §4.6 Compliance

**Commission Adjustment.** Any modification to calculated compensation arising from a refund, chargeback, correction of an error, or compliance action. Made under Section 16 and shown in the Back Office against the transaction that caused them.

**Forfeiture.** The loss of eligibility for compensation, which may occur only on one of the grounds enumerated in §19.4.

**Audit.** A review by the Company to verify compliance with this Plan, including verification of Rank qualification, customer legitimacy, placement and volume.

**KYC.** Know-Your-Customer identity verification. Every person enrolling as an IBO completes KYC, and no funds may be moved out of an account until a unique, verified KYC record is held for that account (§14.4).

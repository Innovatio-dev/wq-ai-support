# Customer acquisition programs: Refer 4, Customer Launch Bonus, Fast 5

The three programs all pay on retail sales to Customers. None of them pays for the act of enrolling or sponsoring anyone. Each is paid **in addition to**, not instead of, the Direct Bonus already earned under Section 8 on the same qualifying sale.

Summary table (derived from §11.1–§11.3; the text below governs):

| Program | Trigger | Award | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refer 4, Pay No More | 4+ personally referred Verified/Independent Customers on the same product line you subscribe to, within a rolling 31-day window | Gold Points equal to the lowest of the four qualifying subscription values, issued as an activation code (expires 40 days) | Evaluated at the close of each Weekly Commission Period |
| Customer Launch Bonus | A personally sponsored IBO makes 4 qualifying sales to 4 distinct Verified/Independent Customers, each ≥ 300 BV (min. 1,200 BV), within 31 days | $200 | On verification |
| Fast 5 Bonus | 5 unique Verified/Independent Customers whose first purchase through your link alone carries ≥ 500 BV | $500 per five qualifying Customers | On verification |

## §11.1 Refer 4, Pay No More

**The idea.** If you personally refer four Customers who each subscribe to the same product you subscribe to, WealthQuest credits you with Gold Points you can put toward your own next subscription of that product line. Because the award equals the lowest of the four qualifying subscription values, it covers your own next subscription in full only where your own subscription is priced at or below that amount. Read this alongside the rule in §15.4 that Gold Points cannot be applied in part.

**How it qualifies.** Within a rolling 31-day window, evaluated at the close of each Weekly Commission Period, you must have four or more personally referred Verified/Independent Customers who have each placed a qualifying order for the same product line in which you hold an active subscription.

**The award.** Gold Points equal to the lowest of the four qualifying subscription values, credited to your Gold Points balance and redeemable against your own subscription of that product line.

**Per product line.** The program runs separately on each product line, once that line is live under an accepted Product Appendix (§2.3). At launch, WealthVentures is the only live product line, so four WealthVentures Customers earn Gold Points toward your WealthVentures subscription. As each additional product line is added, the program extends to it automatically on the same basis — Customers referred on one product line never count toward another.

**Counting rules.**
- Each Customer counts once per evaluation window and toward one product line only.
- Where a Customer has placed more than one qualifying order for the relevant product line within the window, the highest-BV qualifying order is used.
- A Customer must be a unique, valid individual who meets the Verified/Independent Customer definition (§4.1).
- A maximum of one award per product line may be issued to an IBO per weekly evaluation.

**Issued as an activation code, not a Gold Points balance credit.** To avoid confusion with the Gold Points component of ordinary weekly compensation, the award is issued as a redeemable activation code. Every activation code carries the same value and the same conditions as the Gold Points it represents, and is subject to §15.4 in full: non-transferable, not redeemable for cash, generating no BV and no commission for anyone when used, and subject to the monthly redemption limits in §15.4. It cannot be applied in part: if the order value exceeds the activation code's value, it cannot be used for that order at all. All activation codes carry the same conditions, regardless of the IBO or product line.

**This award expires; the 10% Gold Points component of ordinary compensation does not.** A Refer 4 activation code is a time-limited promotional credit and expires **forty (40) days** after issuance if unredeemed. This is the only promotional credit under this Plan with an expiry — Compensation Gold Points remain in your account indefinitely (§15.2).

**Reversal.** If a qualifying Customer order is refunded or charged back, the award may be reversed or adjusted, and any Gold Points already redeemed give rise to a Negative Balance (§16.3).

## §11.2 Customer Launch Bonus

The name and mechanics reflect what it pays on: retail sales volume produced for Customers, by a newly sponsored IBO, during that IBO's first 31 days.

> This bonus is not paid for sponsoring anyone. Sponsoring an IBO who produces no qualifying Customer sales produces no Customer Launch Bonus, in any amount, ever. The bonus is triggered only by verified retail sales to four distinct Verified/Independent Customers, and it is $0 until those sales exist.

- **Qualification.** Earned when an IBO you personally sponsored generates, within thirty-one (31) days of the date their account became an IBO account, at least four qualifying retail sales to four distinct Verified/Independent Customers, each order carrying at least **300 BV** — a minimum of **1,200 BV** of verified retail sales.
- **Amount.** **$200**, paid in the composition set out in Section 15.
- **The 31-day clock.** Runs from the date the account became an IBO account. Where a Customer account is upgraded to an IBO account, the clock starts on the date of that upgrade, not on the date the Customer account was opened.
- **Once per sponsored IBO.** You may earn this bonus once per personally sponsored IBO. It is not cumulative and only the qualifying event is paid.
- **Timing and verification.** Paid on successful verification of the qualifying sales, which may involve validation and processing delay. If any qualifying order is refunded, charged back or invalidated, the bonus is reversed and offset against future earnings (§16).

## §11.3 Fast 5 Bonus

Not limited to any single product line.

- **Qualification.** Five unique Verified/Independent Customers each complete a first purchase, through your referral link, that alone carries at least **500 BV**. What qualifies is the total BV of their first purchase, not which product line it came from — but at launch, WealthVentures is the only live product line (§2.3), so that BV can currently only come from WealthVentures products. As additional product lines are added, the threshold may be reached by a single product or by any combination of live product lines within that one order.
- **Amount.** **$500 for every five qualifying Customers**, paid in the composition set out in Section 15.
- **Repeatable without limit.** No cap on how many times it may be earned. Each award requires a fresh set of five unique Customers, each qualifying on their own first purchase; a Customer counted toward one award may not be counted again toward another, and a Customer's second or later purchase does not itself qualify them.
- **Reversal.** If a qualifying order is refunded or charged back within the applicable window, the award is reversed and offset against future earnings.

## §11.4 Rules common to all three programs

- Customers must be legitimate, unique individuals. Fabricated, duplicated or nominee Customer accounts are a material breach (§17.4).
- Orders placed for the purpose of manufacturing a qualification, rather than for genuine end use, do not count and are grounds for reversal and compliance action.
- The Company may audit any qualification at any time, including by contacting the Customer to verify the sale.
- All three programs require Active IBO status and a current Monthly License Fee at the time of qualification.

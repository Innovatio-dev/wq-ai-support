# Commission periods, payout, KYC, currency and PayQuicker

## §14.1 The Weekly Commission Period

Begins **Tuesday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time** and ends the following **Monday at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time**. All weekly income streams — Direct Bonus, SITB and Generational Match — are calculated on this period.

## §14.2 Calculation and Payout Date

Calculation begins when the administrator closes the period on Monday night. Eligible Earnings (§4.5) for the period are released to the IBO's Wallet on the Payout Date: **Friday at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time** of the same week. Amounts released to the Wallet are available for withdrawal subject to verification (§14.4) and to any Commission Adjustment arising under Section 16.

## §14.3 Monthly and annual awards

- **Leadership Rank Bonus:** paid monthly, on the second Friday of each month (no later than the 15th) for all qualifying IBOs, with the first installment falling in the month after qualification (§12.4).
- **Ambassador Travel Bonus:** issued as travel credit on the first Friday of February following the Annual Qualification Period (§13.5).

Summary table (derived from §8.7, §11, §12.4, §13.5, §14.1–§14.3):

| Stream | Calculated | Released |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Bonus, SITB, Generational Match | At close of the Weekly Commission Period (Monday night) | Friday 12:00 a.m. PT of the same week |
| Customer Launch Bonus, Fast 5 Bonus | On verification of the qualifying sales | Paid on verification, 90/10 per §15 |
| Refer 4 | At close of each Weekly Commission Period | As an activation code |
| Leadership Rank Bonus | Monthly | Second Friday of each month (no later than the 15th) |
| Ambassador Travel Bonus | Annually (1 Jan – 31 Dec) | First Friday of February, as travel credit |

## §14.4 KYC and withdrawal

- Every person enrolling as an IBO completes identity verification (KYC) as part of activation. No funds may be moved out of an account until a verified KYC record is held for that account, and each KYC record must be unique: an identity already verified on one account cannot be used to verify a second (§17.3).
- Verification is two-part, and it gates participation, not only withdrawal. Both WealthQuest's own check through **Sumsub** and **PayQuicker's** independent check must clear. Until both have cleared, the person holds Applicant status (§4.1): they are not an IBO, no BV accrues, no Rank is held, and no compensation of any kind accrues. The Applicant's placement position is reserved at payment and activated on verification. Monthly License Fee billing is suspended while Applicant status is held and resumes on verification.
- **What is shared with the Payout Provider.** WealthQuest does not share the output of its own verification process with the Payout Provider. PayQuicker runs its own independent verification. WealthQuest shares only the basic account information needed to open a payout account: name, email address and country.

## §14.5 Currency and conversion

BV is tracked strictly in points and is never converted between currencies. Compensation is calculated in **US dollars** and credited to your Wallet on that basis. Where withdrawal in a different local currency is needed, that conversion happens once, at the point of payout execution, at the actual rate provided by the Payout Provider at that time. The Company does not apply an internal rate, does not fix a rate earlier in the cycle, and does not adjust the provider's rate in its own favour (Agreement, Section 8). WealthQuest, LLC is the Company's sole Contracting Entity today; if additional Contracting Entities are formed, this Section will be updated to describe any resulting currency or conversion mechanics.

## §14.6 Taxes

You are solely responsible for all taxes arising from compensation paid under this Plan. The Company does not provide tax advice and does not determine your reporting obligations on your behalf (Agreement, Section 3).

## §14.7 Your payout provider

- WealthQuest uses **PayQuicker** as its designated global payout provider. PayQuicker serves every IBO worldwide on the same basis — there is no separate regional rail — and will continue to do so regardless of which Contracting Entity an IBO contracts with.
- **Sanctions.** WealthQuest offers the opportunity and its products and services worldwide, without geographic restriction, except that it does not knowingly enroll, sell to, or pay compensation to any individual or entity located in, or who is a national of, a country or territory subject to comprehensive sanctions administered by the U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), the European Union, the United Nations Security Council, or the Swiss State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO), or that WealthQuest's payment or banking partners decline to support for equivalent sanctions-compliance reasons — consistent with the sanctions screening in the KYC/AML Statement, Section 5. PayQuicker's worldwide coverage is therefore not a guarantee of service to every country: an applicant located in a sanctioned jurisdiction cannot be paid and cannot become or remain an IBO.
- **How it works.** Compensation is calculated at period close and credited to your Wallet in the Back Office on the Payout Date. From the Wallet you withdraw the cash component to your PayQuicker account, from which you may spend or transfer it under PayQuicker's own terms, according to your own settings and the payout options PayQuicker makes available to you.
- Withdrawal requires a verified, unique KYC record (§14.4), and the identity verified with WealthQuest must match the identity held by PayQuicker.
- **Currency.** Where a cross-currency conversion is required, it is performed once at payout execution at PayQuicker's actual rate at that moment (§14.5).
- **Provider terms and fees.** Your PayQuicker account is governed by your own agreement with PayQuicker. Any account, transfer or withdrawal fee, any minimum withdrawal amount, and any limit PayQuicker applies are set by PayQuicker and not by WealthQuest, and are disclosed at the point of account setup. WealthQuest does not receive any share of a fee PayQuicker charges you.
- **Everything that is not Gold Points moves through PayQuicker.** The entire cash share of every payment — 90% by default — is credited to your Wallet and withdrawn to PayQuicker. Gold Points never leave the Back Office at all (§15.4).
- **If the provider changes.** The Company may designate an additional or replacement payout provider — for example, to add local rails in a new Territory — on reasonable advance notice through the Back Office and by email (Agreement, Section 19.1). A change of provider does not alter what you are owed, how it is calculated, or when it is released; it changes only the route by which you receive it.

# Payment composition (90% cash / 10% Gold Points) and Gold Points rules

## §15.0 Composition

Every money payment — Direct Bonus, SITB, Generational Match, Customer Launch Bonus, Fast 5 Bonus and Leadership Rank Bonus — is paid in two parts: **90% cash (US dollars) and 10% Gold Points**, with no election between components. The composition does not vary by IBO or by Territory, and is fixed at 90/10 for every payment **except** the half, unbranded Leadership Rank Bonus installment (§12.3), which is paid entirely in cash with no Gold Points component.

| Component | Share | Form and destination |
|---|---|---|
| Cash | 90% | US dollars, credited to your Wallet and withdrawable to PayQuicker (§14.7) — converted to your local currency at withdrawal where applicable — according to your own PayQuicker settings and available options |
| Gold Points | 10% | Internal reward credit, held in your Wallet (§15.2) |

**Worked example.** You earn $100 of commission for a period. It is paid to you as $90 in cash and 10 Gold Points credited to your Wallet.

The Ambassador Travel Bonus is issued as travel credit and is not subject to this composition.

## §15.1 Gold Points — what they are

- Live now — an ordinary part of weekly compensation, not a future or conditional feature — and unrelated to GoldMind and Liquid Gold (§4.5).
- An internal reward credit that works like an airline mileage award: a reward you can apply to a later purchase inside the WealthQuest ecosystem. **One Gold Point represents one US dollar of redemption value.**
- Issued in two classes: Compensation Gold Points (§15.2) and Reward Gold Points (§15.3). Both are held in your Wallet and both redeem on the terms in §15.4; they differ only in how they are issued and in what happens to them if your account lapses.
- GoldMind and Liquid Gold are unrelated to Gold Points, are not cryptocurrency, digital-asset, or precious-metal-backed products, and neither has a Product Appendix, is available for enrollment, or generates compensation of any kind until each separately completes its own compliance review and goes live.

## §15.2 Compensation Gold Points

The 10% Gold Points share of IBO compensation (§15.0). They are earned compensation. They **never expire**: they remain in your account until you redeem them, including if you do not sign in for an extended period. They are **never forfeited** — not on lapse of the Monthly License Fee, not on cancellation, not on non-renewal, and not on suspension.

## §15.3 Reward Gold Points

Issued with a WealthVentures membership Activation payment or monthly Subscription payment, together with promotional awards including the Refer 4 award (§11.1). On account lapse — cancellation, non-payment or suspension — Reward Gold Points are **held for ninety (90) days**, restored in full if the account is reactivated within that window, and forfeited on the 90th day if it is not.

A Refer 4 award keeps its separate life: it is issued as an activation code rather than as a credit to your Gold Points balance and expires **forty (40) days** after issuance if unredeemed (§11.1). It is the only promotional credit under this Plan with an expiry of its own.

Summary table (derived from §11.1, §15.2 and §15.3):

| Class | Source | On lapse | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compensation Gold Points | 10% of every money payment | Never forfeited | Never |
| Reward Gold Points | Activation / Subscription payments, promotional awards | Held 90 days; restored on reactivation, forfeited on day 90 | — |
| Refer 4 activation code | Refer 4 award | — | 40 days after issuance if unredeemed |

## §15.4 Redemption, monthly limits, and what Gold Points are not

**What Gold Points can be used for:** buying WealthQuest products; paying for a WealthQuest subscription; performing an upgrade; and purchasing event tickets. Additional redemption options may be introduced from time to time; their terms are published separately.

Gold Points of both classes:
- have no cash value and are not money, a security, a stored-value instrument, a deposit or a claim against the Company;
- can never be redeemed for cash, cashed out, or converted into any currency or cryptocurrency, under any circumstance;
- cannot be transferred, gifted, sold, or assigned to any other IBO, Customer, or third party, or moved between accounts, under any circumstance;
- may be redeemed only for the goods, services, subscriptions, upgrades, events and credits WealthQuest makes available for Gold Point redemption from time to time;
- are not linked to the market price of gold, any other commodity, or any cryptocurrency;
- generate no BV and no commission for anyone when used — an order paid in whole or in part with Gold Points is not a Commissionable Event; and
- cannot be applied in part: if the order value exceeds your available Gold Points balance, Gold Points cannot be used for that order at all.

**Monthly redemption limits.** Across all Gold Points held, regardless of how they were issued, an IBO may redeem in any calendar month no more than:

| Product | Maximum redeemable per calendar month |
|---|---|
| WealthVentures subscription | 1 |

These limits apply to subscriptions and memberships. They do not cap the redemption of Gold Points against event tickets or upgrades. At launch, WealthVentures is the only live product line, so it is the only row; as each additional product line is added (§2.3), its own monthly redemption limit will be added at that time.

Because Compensation Gold Points do not expire but can only be redeemed at the rate above, a balance may build up faster than it can be spent. Gold Points are a genuine benefit, but they are not the same thing as cash and should not be counted as though they were.

Gold Points are a compensation and redemption mechanic. They are not a security, a deposit or an investment product, they confer no ownership interest of any kind, and they carry no right to any return, yield or appreciation.

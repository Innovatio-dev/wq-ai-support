# Definitions: participants and status

Terms are grouped by subject. A term used in this Plan that is not defined here or in the IBO Agreement carries its plain, ordinary meaning.

**IBO (Independent Business Owner).** An individual who has enrolled under the IBO Agreement and holds one of the statuses defined in Section 4 of that Agreement. Used as the umbrella term for all such individuals regardless of status.

**Applicant.** A person who has accepted the IBO Agreement and paid, but whose verification has not yet cleared.
- Verification is two-part: both WealthQuest's own check through **Sumsub** and **PayQuicker's** independent check must clear.
- An Applicant is not an IBO: no Back Office access beyond completing verification, no Rank, and no BV and no compensation accrues.
- The placement position is reserved at payment and activated on verification, so tree position follows enrollment order rather than verification speed.
- Further Monthly License Fee billing is suspended while a person holds Applicant status and resumes on verification.
- Applicant status has a **thirty (30) day** outer limit; if verification has not cleared by then, the application is deemed declined, and the declined-application refund rule in Section 3 of the IBO Agreement applies.

**Customer.** A person who purchases WealthQuest products or services for personal use and who is not an IBO, an Enrolled — Pending Activation or Enrolled — Paused Activation participant, or a Brand Advocate. A Customer has no rights under this Plan and is never counted, for any reporting, income-disclosure or compliance purpose, as a participant at any status level or Rank.

**Verified/Independent Customer.** A Customer who (a) is not an IBO, an Enrolled participant of either kind, or a Brand Advocate; (b) is not the referring IBO's spouse or household member; and (c) did not purchase primarily to help the referring IBO qualify for activation, a Rank, or any compensation. Only a sale to a Verified/Independent Customer counts toward Active IBO status, Qualified IBO status, or any customer-count requirement in this Plan.

**Brand Advocate.** A no-fee, no-commission, referral-only status (Agreement, Section 4.2). A Brand Advocate earns nothing under this Plan and may not recruit or sponsor.

**Enrolled — Pending Activation.** A person who has accepted the Agreement, paid the License Fee and completed onboarding, but has not yet made a first verified retail sale. Earns nothing under this Plan and may not sponsor or recruit (Agreement, Section 4.3). Both restrictions lift on the first verified retail sale, which is itself commissionable (§8.2).

**Enrolled — Paused Activation.** An IBO who previously held Active or Qualified status but whose Monthly License Fee has lapsed. Earns nothing while paused; account, KYC record, tree position, sales history and Rank are fully preserved.

**Active IBO.** An IBO who, at the relevant time, satisfies all four conditions in Section 4.4 of the Agreement: (1) the Agreement is in effect; (2) the Monthly License Fee is current; (3) onboarding is complete; and (4) at least one verified retail sale has been made to a Verified/Independent Customer. Eligible for the Direct Bonus and the customer acquisition programs in Section 11.

**Qualified IBO.** An Active IBO who has also made at least one verified retail sale on each leg of the Placement Tree — one left, one right (Agreement, Section 4.5). Qualified status is the eligibility gate for every rank-based income stream: SITB, Generational Match, the Leadership Rank Bonus and the Ambassador Travel Bonus. It is a gate, not an amount: what you are paid in a given period is determined separately, by your Rank (Section 6).

**Qualified IBO — Unranked.** A Qualified IBO who, on a given day, does not meet the volume and structural thresholds for Bronze Associate (Rank 1). The IBO retains Qualified status and every right attached to it, but accrues no SITB for that day. This Plan does not use the term "Rank 0."

**Sponsor.** The IBO whose referral link or IBO ID a new enrollee identifies as the source of their enrollment. **Personally Sponsored** means enrolled directly under your Sponsor ID, and is measured in the Enroller Tree.

**Good Standing.** An IBO is in Good Standing at a given time if, and only if, all of the following are true:
- (a) they hold Active IBO status;
- (b) their Monthly License Fee is current;
- (c) no compliance violation has been established against them following investigation that remains unremedied; and
- (d) they are not in breach of a written compliance directive issued by the Company.

Good Standing is determined only on these four criteria. No other consideration — including subjective assessment of conduct, attitude or alignment with Company values — is applied to any decision that affects an IBO's compensation.

Two things expressly **do not** remove Good Standing:
- An **open investigation**: compensation during an investigation is held and then released or resolved under §19.3, not stopped at source.
- An **outstanding Negative Balance**: it is offset against future compensation under §16.3, which requires that compensation to keep accruing.

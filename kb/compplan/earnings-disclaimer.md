# Earnings disclaimer and related documents

Sections 21.1 and 21.2 state what the figures in this Plan are and are not. Sections 21.3 to 21.5 point to the standalone documents that govern earnings disclosure, marketing conduct and personal data.

## §21.1 No income is guaranteed

- WealthQuest does not guarantee that any IBO will reach any Rank, earn any amount, or recover the cost of participation.
- Earning income as an IBO is not typical, is not guaranteed, and requires meaningful personal effort. Most people who participate in direct selling earn little or no money, and some lose money once fees and expenses are taken into account.
- Operating a WealthQuest business requires the fees in §5.1 ($99 Activation Fee, $29 Monthly License Fee), plus any marketing, advertising, travel or event costs you choose to incur, and those costs can exceed your gross earnings — a realistic possible outcome, not a remote one.
- WealthQuest is a newly launched company. As of the date of this Plan it has **no earnings history of any kind** — no average, no median, no top-earner figure, and no percentage of participants who earned anything — because no one has yet had the opportunity to earn.
- Every new IBO has **fourteen (14) calendar days** from enrollment to cancel for any reason and receive a full refund of fees paid (Agreement, Section 6).

## §21.2 Every figure in this Plan is an illustration

Every number, table, percentage and example exists to show how the Plan's arithmetic works. Nothing in it is a projection, a forecast, a promise or a representation about what any real person has earned or is likely to earn. The illustrative 7-day and 30-day columns in §9.3, the annual figures in §13.2, and the travel examples in §13.7 all assume uninterrupted Rank maintenance, which is an assumption made for the purpose of demonstrating the calculation and not an expectation. The same applies to the worked examples in §9.5, §10.3, §13.5 and §15.0.

## §21.3 The Income Disclosure Statement

A separate published document and the Company's authoritative source for earnings information. It sets out what an individual can realistically expect to earn, the pre-launch position on earnings data, the Company's commitment on when and how actual figures will be published, and the conditions under which an IBO may share their own results. It is incorporated into the IBO Agreement by reference and is binding in the same way this Plan is.

Read it before enrolling: https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/income-disclosure

No IBO may make an earnings claim that is inconsistent with, or not substantiated by, that Statement. The rules on what you may and may not say about income live there and in Section 4.2 of the Policies and Procedures — not in this Plan.

## §21.4 The Policies and Procedures

The operational rulebook for how you conduct your business day to day, including how you may market and advertise, what claims are prohibited, how Company trademarks may be used, how sponsorship and placement disputes are handled, and what records you must keep. Incorporated into the IBO Agreement by reference and binding in the same way this Plan is.

Read them before enrolling: https://www.wealthquest.com/legal/policies-procedures

## §21.6 Not advice

This Plan is informational. It is not investment, business, financial, tax or legal advice. Prospective IBOs are encouraged to evaluate the opportunity carefully, including with their own independent advisor, before enrolling.

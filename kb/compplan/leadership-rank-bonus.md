# Leadership Rank Bonus

## §12.1 Purpose

Recognizes sustained leadership performance: a monthly bonus paid over up to twelve months to an IBO who has demonstrated that a Rank of 5 or above is held consistently rather than momentarily.

The vehicle-branding condition is a **marketing arrangement, not a vehicle purchase or lease reimbursement**. The Company pays the full monthly installment to an IBO who displays Company-approved branding on a qualifying personal vehicle, because that display is a marketing service the Company is paying for. An IBO who does not is still paid — half of each installment, in cash, with no vehicle condition attached to that reduced amount. The bonus is never conditioned on owning, leasing or buying any vehicle.

## §12.2 Qualification — the Sustained Rank Standard

- Achieve the Rank, or higher, as Weekly Rank in **four (4) of the last eight (8)** Weekly Commission Periods. The four periods need not be consecutive.
- Throughout the qualification period, maintain Qualified IBO status, a current Monthly License Fee and Good Standing, and the qualifying Rank must have been achieved through compliant and verified volume and structure.
- The Company may audit qualification before the first installment is paid.

## §12.3 Amounts and the vehicle-branding condition

- **Branded — full installment.** The IBO owns or leases a qualifying vehicle — model year within the last **five (5) years**, of a premium make as listed in the Company's Vehicle Branding Guidelines in the Back Office — and has placed Company-approved branding on it as the Guidelines specify. The IBO receives the full installment, in the composition set out in Section 15.
- **Unbranded — half installment, cash.** The condition is not met that month, for any reason: no qualifying vehicle, a vehicle that does not meet the age or make requirement, or a qualifying vehicle left unbranded. The IBO receives **50%** of the installment, paid entirely in cash with no Gold Points component, and with no vehicle, lease or purchase requirement of any kind attached to it.

| Rank | Full (branded) installment | Half (unbranded) installment | Maximum over 12 installments, branded throughout |
|---|---|---|---|
| R5 Silver Manager | $600 | $300 | $7,200 |
| R6 Gold Manager | $800 | $400 | $9,600 |
| R7 Bronze Executive | $1,200 | $600 | $14,400 |
| R8 Silver Executive | $2,000 | $1,000 | $24,000 |
| R9 Gold Executive | $3,000 | $1,500 | $36,000 |
| R10 Bronze Ambassador | $3,500 | $1,750 | $42,000 |
| R11 Silver Ambassador | $5,000 | $2,500 | $60,000 |
| R12 Gold Ambassador | $8,000 | $4,000 | $96,000 |

The right-hand column is the maximum payable if all twelve installments are paid at the full, branded rate. It is not guaranteed and it is not a typical outcome.

"Premium make" and the branding specification are set out in the Vehicle Branding Guidelines, published in the Back Office and not reproduced in the Plan, so they can be updated without amending the Plan. Changing them does not require the notice in Section 20, because they do not change any bonus percentage, threshold or amount — only which vehicles qualify for the full rate.

## §12.4 How the bonus is paid

- Qualification unlocks a schedule of up to **twelve consecutive monthly installments**.
- Installments are paid to all qualifying IBOs on the **second Friday of each month**, which falls no later than the 15th of that month. An IBO who qualifies in one month receives their first installment in the following month.
- Each installment is payable only if, on the installment date, the IBO holds Qualified IBO status and is in Good Standing. An installment falling in a month in which the IBO does not meet those conditions is not paid, is not made up later, and does not extend the schedule beyond twelve months from the first installment.
- **Verifying the vehicle-branding condition.** Before the first installment, and at any time afterward, the Company may require proof that a qualifying vehicle carries Company-approved branding — ordinarily current photographs of the vehicle together with confirmation of its model year and make. Whether the full or half rate applies is determined by whether that proof is current as of the installment date. An IBO may move between the full and half rate from one month to the next; doing so has no effect on Rank, Good Standing, or any other income stream, and is never treated as a compliance issue.

## §12.5 Advancing to a higher Rank

If an IBO meets the Sustained Rank Standard at a higher Rank while a schedule is running, the existing schedule stops and a new twelve-installment schedule begins at the higher amount. Unpaid installments from the lower Rank are forfeited and are never recovered, whether or not the IBO later returns to the lower Rank.

## §12.6 Once per Rank

Each Rank's Leadership Rank Bonus may be earned **once per IBO per lifetime**. An IBO who has completed or forfeited a schedule at a given Rank does not qualify for a second schedule at that same Rank.

## §12.7 No guarantee

The number of installments actually paid depends on continued eligibility under §12.4. No installment is guaranteed, and the bonus is subject to reversal and adjustment on the same basis as every other payment (§16 and §19).

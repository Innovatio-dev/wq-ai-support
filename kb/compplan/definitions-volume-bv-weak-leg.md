# Definitions: Business Volume, weak leg and Volume Window

## Business Volume ("BV")

- The point value assigned by the Company to each commissionable product and service.
- BV attaches to the product, not to a price or a currency: the same product carries the same BV in every market and in every currency in which it is sold, and **no exchange-rate conversion is ever applied to BV**.
- Current BV assignments are published in the **Product & BV Schedule** in the Back Office and may be changed only under Section 20.

**BV is generated only by** a completed, paid and verified retail sale of a commissionable product or service to a Customer, made through the selling IBO's referral link, while that IBO's Monthly License Fee is current.

**BV is never generated by:**
- the Activation Fee;
- the Monthly License Fee;
- an IBO's own personal purchase for personal use;
- any order paid in whole or in part with Gold Points;
- administrative or shipping charges; or
- any item the Product & BV Schedule identifies as non-commissionable.

## Weak Leg Volume

On any given day, the Weak Leg is the leg of the Placement Tree — left or right — having the lesser Business Volume within the Volume Window ending on that day, being the trailing thirty-one (31) calendar days. Weak Leg Volume is the aggregate BV attributed to that leg within that window. Leg designation — weak versus strong — is determined afresh each day, and the leg that is weak on one day may be strong on the next.

## Strong Leg Volume

On any given day, the Strong Leg is the leg having the greater Business Volume within the same Volume Window, determined on the same basis. Strong Leg Volume is tracked and remains valid only while inside its own rolling thirty-one (31) day Volume Window, and expires with that volume.

Because both figures are trailing 31-day totals recalculated each day, they are **never summed across the days of a week**: the figure used for any given day is that day's own 31-day total.

All leg volume is subject to verification, to the rolling 31-day window, and to adjustment for returns, refunds, chargebacks or compliance action.

## Volume Window

The rolling thirty-one (31) calendar days ending on the day of calculation. BV enters the window on the day the underlying sale completes and leaves it on the thirty-second day.
